# Module M03 — Recent or distant?

> **Level**: Inter (B1)
> **Block**: B1 Block
> **Units**: 5 (I01–I05)
> **Theme**: Present perfect vs past simple — when to use each
> **Module goal**: Student distinguishes PP (life experience, connected to present) from past simple (finished time), and uses for/since correctly.
> **Connects to**: M04 (used to) — after mastering PP, learn past habits.

## Module chunks introduced (~25)

- for + duration (for two years, for an hour)
- since + point in time (since 2010, since Monday)
- How long have you...?
- ago (past simple) vs since (PP)
- yesterday/last week → past simple
- this week/today → present perfect (still relevant)

## Unit I01 — Finished or still going?

> **Sub-CEFR**: B1 | **Grammar focus**: PP for connected past vs past simple for finished | **Tense**: PRESENT · perfect vs past
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: You distinguish what's finished from what's still relevant.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ Italy last year."
   **Options**: visited / have visited / had visited
   **Answer**: visited
   **Explanation**: "Last year" = finished time → past simple. "I visited Italy last year".

2. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ Italy three times in my life."
   **Options**: have visited / visited / had visited
   **Answer**: have visited
   **Explanation**: "In my life" = experience, no specific time → present perfect.

3. **word_bank** — "She _____ this morning."
   **Choices**: called / has called / had called / is calling
   **Answer**: has called
   **Explanation**: "This morning" (if it's still morning) = unfinished → PP. If morning is over → past simple "called".

4. **word_bank** — "We _____ yesterday."
   **Choices**: met / have met / had met / are meeting
   **Answer**: met
   **Explanation**: "Yesterday" = finished → past simple.

5. **fill_gap** — "I _____ him in 2010 and I _____ him many times since."
   **Hint**: 1st = met (2010 specific), 2nd = have met
   **Answer**: met
   **Explanation**: "In 2010" = specific past → "met". (Second blank would be "have seen/met").

6. **fill_gap** — "She _____ this year." (job stayed)
   **Hint**: PP for unfinished period
   **Answer**: has worked
   **Explanation**: "This year" not finished → PP "has worked".

7. **fill_gap** — "He _____ Brazil three years ago."
   **Hint**: ago = past simple
   **Answer**: visited
   **Explanation**: "Ago" always with past simple. "Visited Brazil three years ago".

8. **fix_error** — "I have seen her yesterday."
   **Hint**: yesterday + PP doesn't mix
   **Answer**: I saw her yesterday.
   **Explanation**: "Yesterday" = specific finished time → past simple, not PP.

9. **fix_error** — "I visited many countries in my life."
   **Hint**: "In my life" suggests PP
   **Answer**: I have visited many countries in my life.
   **Explanation**: "In my life" = no specific time, experience → PP.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I went to Italy last year. I've visited it three times in my life. I haven't been there yet this year."
    **Question**: When did the speaker go to Italy specifically?
    **Answer**: last year
    **Explanation**: The text says "I went to Italy last year".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I visited Italy last year."** — past simple specific
2. **"I have visited Italy three times."** — PP experience
3. **"She called yesterday."** — past simple
4. **"She has called this morning."** — PP unfinished
5. **"We met in 2010."** — past simple

## Unit I02 — For two years

   **Explanation**: "For + duration" — period of time. "For two years".

2. **multiple_choice** — "She has worked there _____ a long time."
   **Options**: for / since / during
   **Answer**: for
   **Explanation**: "For a long time" — duration.

3. **word_bank** — "We have been friends _____ years."
