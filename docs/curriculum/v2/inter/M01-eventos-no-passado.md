# Module M01 — Past events

> **Level**: Inter (B1)
> **Block**: B1 Block
> **Units**: 5 (I01–I05)
> **Theme**: Past continuous + narrative contrast with past simple
> **Module goal**: Student can describe ongoing past actions with past continuous (was/were + -ing), contrast with past simple (background vs event), and use while/when/as in narrative.
> **Connects to**: I02 (Present perfect) — after past tense narratives, move to life experiences with present perfect.

## Module chunks introduced (~30)

- I was working / She was studying / They were sleeping
- I wasn't working / He wasn't watching
- Was I working? / Were you sleeping?
- while / when / as (time clauses)
- It was raining when I left.
- While I was cooking, she called.
- past continuous = background, past simple = event
- at that moment / at the time / yesterday morning

## Unit I01 — What were you doing?

> **Sub-CEFR**: B1 | **Grammar focus**: Past continuous positive (was/were + verb-ing) | **Tense**: PAST · continuous
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: You describe what you were doing at a specific moment yesterday.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ studying at 8pm yesterday."
   **Options**: was / were / am
   **Answer**: was
   **Explanation**: Past continuous with "I" uses "was" + verb-ing: "I was studying".

2. **multiple_choice** — "They _____ playing soccer when it rained."
   **Options**: were / was / are
   **Answer**: were
   **Explanation**: Plurals (they/we/you) use "were" + ing: "They were playing".

3. **word_bank** — "She _____ cooking dinner at 7pm."
   **Choices**: was / were / is / had
   **Answer**: was
   **Explanation**: She/he/it use "was" in past continuous.

4. **word_bank** — "We _____ watching TV last night."
   **Choices**: were / was / are / had
   **Answer**: were
   **Explanation**: "We" plural uses "were watching".

5. **fill_gap** — "At 10pm yesterday, I _____ sleeping."
   **Hint**: was/were for "I"
   **Answer**: was
   **Explanation**: "I was sleeping" — past continuous with I.

6. **fill_gap** — "They _____ talking when I arrived."
   **Hint**: was/were for plural
   **Answer**: were
   **Explanation**: "They were talking" — past continuous plural.

7. **fill_gap** — "He was _____ his car with soap when I called."
   **Hint**: verb "wash" in -ing form
   **Answer**: washing
   **Explanation**: "Wash" + ing = "washing". "With soap" makes it clear the verb is "wash" (not "fix" / "drive"). Note: simple -ing, no doubled consonant.

8. **fix_error** — "I were studying yesterday."
   **Hint**: I uses "was", not "were"
   **Answer**: I was studying yesterday.
   **Explanation**: "I" uses "was" in past tense, not "were". Only we/you/they use "were".

9. **fix_error** — "She was cook dinner."
   **Hint**: After "was", verb needs -ing
   **Answer**: She was cooking dinner.
   **Explanation**: Past continuous = was/were + verb-ING. "Cooking", not "cook".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "Yesterday at 8pm, I was studying English. My sister was watching TV. My parents were eating dinner. We were all busy."
    **Question**: What was the sister doing?
    **Answer**: watching TV
    **Explanation**: The text says "My sister was watching TV".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I was studying at 8pm."** — own past continuous
2. **"They were playing soccer."** — plural past continuous
3. **"She was cooking dinner."** — 3rd person
4. **"We were watching TV last night."** — group activity
5. **"He was washing his car when I called."** — past continuous + when

2. **multiple_choice** — "They _____ playing soccer; they were watching TV."

7. **fill_gap** — "They _____ paying attention."

## Unit I04 — While I was cooking

> **Sub-CEFR**: B1 | **Grammar focus**: Past continuous + past simple (while/when/as) | **Tense**: PAST · continuous
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: You tell stories where one action interrupts another in the past.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "_____ I was cooking, she called."
   **Options**: While / Because / So
   **Answer**: While
   **Explanation**: "While" introduces a long action in progress. "While I was cooking, she called" — background + event.

2. **multiple_choice** — "She was sleeping _____ the phone rang."
   **Options**: when / while / because
   **Answer**: when
   **Explanation**: "When" introduces the shorter, interrupting action: "She was sleeping when the phone rang".

3. **word_bank** — "I _____ TV when he arrived."
   **Choices**: was watching / watched / watch / had watched
   **Answer**: was watching
   **Explanation**: Background action (longer) = past continuous: "I was watching". Event (shorter) = past simple: "he arrived".

4. **word_bank** — "While she _____, I made dinner."
   **Choices**: was working / worked / works / had worked
   **Answer**: was working
   **Explanation**: After "while", use past continuous: "while she was working".

5. **fill_gap** — "I was reading when my friend _____."
   **Hint**: past simple of "call"
   **Answer**: called
   **Explanation**: Event (interruption) = past simple: "called".

6. **fill_gap** — "While she _____ TV, the lights went off."
   **Hint**: past continuous of "watch"
   **Answer**: was watching
   **Explanation**: Background = past continuous: "was watching".

7. **fill_gap** — "As I _____ home, I saw an accident."
   **Hint**: past continuous of "walk"
   **Answer**: was walking
   **Explanation**: "As" similar to "while" — background action.

8. **fix_error** — "I was cooking when she was arriving."
   **Hint**: Event = past simple, not continuous
   **Answer**: I was cooking when she arrived.
   **Explanation**: Interrupting event is past SIMPLE (arrived), not continuous. "When she arrived".

9. **fix_error** — "While I cooked, she called."
   **Hint**: After while, past continuous
   **Answer**: While I was cooking, she called.
   **Explanation**: After "while", use past continuous (the longer action). "While I was cooking".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "Yesterday I was studying when my friend called. While I was talking to her, my mom arrived. We were having dinner when the lights went off."
    **Question**: What was the speaker doing when the friend called?
    **Answer**: studying
    **Explanation**: The text says "I was studying when my friend called".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

## Unit I05 — Tell the full story

> **Sub-CEFR**: B1 | **Grammar focus**: Integration past continuous + past simple in narrative | **Tense**: PAST · continuous
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: You tell a full short story combining past continuous (background) and past simple (events).

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ when she arrived."
   **Options**: was studying / studied / studies
   **Answer**: was studying
   **Explanation**: Background action = past continuous.

2. **multiple_choice** — "She _____ when I called."
   **Options**: was working / worked / works
   **Answer**: was working
   **Explanation**: Background = continuous.

3. **word_bank** — "We _____ dinner when the doorbell rang."
   **Choices**: were having / had / have / are having
   **Answer**: were having
   **Explanation**: "Were having" — background dinner action.

4. **word_bank** — "While he _____, the alarm went off."
   **Choices**: was sleeping / slept / sleeps / had slept
   **Answer**: was sleeping
   **Explanation**: After while, past continuous.

5. **fill_gap** — "I _____ TV when my mom came in."
   **Hint**: Background action
   **Answer**: was watching
   **Explanation**: "Was watching" — past continuous background.

6. **fill_gap** — "She _____ the bus when it started to rain."
   **Hint**: past continuous of "wait for"
   **Answer**: was waiting for
   **Explanation**: "Was waiting for the bus" — background.

7. **fill_gap** — "While we _____ for lunch, it started to rain."
   **Hint**: past continuous of "cook"
   **Answer**: were cooking
   **Explanation**: "Were cooking" — plural past continuous.

8. **fix_error** — "When I came, she cooked."
   **Hint**: Wrong tenses for interruption
   **Answer**: When I came, she was cooking.
   **Explanation**: The ongoing action (cooking) should be continuous. "She was cooking when I came".

9. **fix_error** — "While I worked, she called."
   **Hint**: While + past continuous
   **Answer**: While I was working, she called.
   **Explanation**: After while, the longer action is continuous: "While I was working".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "Yesterday I was walking home when it started to rain. I was running to a shop when I saw an old friend. We were talking when our boss called us both."
    **Question**: What was the speaker doing when it started to rain?
    **Answer**: walking home
    **Explanation**: The text says "I was walking home when it started to rain".
