# Module M06 — Possibilities and doubts

> **Level**: Inter (B1)
> **Block**: B1 Block
> **Units**: 5 (I01–I05)
> **Theme**: Modals of possibility (might / may / could / can't)
> **Module goal**: Student speculates about present situations with might/may/could (possibility) and can't (impossibility).
> **Connects to**: M07 (precise quantifiers).

## Module chunks introduced (~20)

- It might rain. / She might come.
- He may be late. / They may be busy.
- It could be true. / She could be at home.
- It can't be true. / He can't be there.
- I think she might... / Maybe he could...

## Unit I01 — It might rain

> **Sub-CEFR**: B1 | **Grammar focus**: might for possibility | **Tense**: MODAL · might
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: You speculate about possibilities for the day.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "It _____ rain tomorrow."
   **Options**: might / will / does
   **Answer**: might
   **Explanation**: "Might + base" = possibility (maybe).

2. **multiple_choice** — "She _____ be late."
   **Options**: might / wills / does
   **Answer**: might
   **Explanation**: Modal "might" — same for all persons.

3. **word_bank** — "We _____ go to the beach."
   **Choices**: might / are might / will be might / mighting
   **Answer**: might
   **Explanation**: Modal, base form after.

4. **word_bank** — "He might _____ home."
   **Choices**: be / is / was / been
   **Answer**: be
   **Explanation**: After "might", base "be".

5. **fill_gap** — "I _____ visit my parents this weekend."
   **Hint**: possibility modal
   **Answer**: might
   **Explanation**: "I might visit" — uncertain plan.

6. **fill_gap** — "She might _____ tired."
   **Hint**: base "to be"
   **Answer**: be
   **Explanation**: "Might be tired" — possibility about state.

7. **fill_gap** — "_____ I borrow your pen?" (asking permission politely)
   **Hint**: polite modal
   **Answer**: Might
   **Accepts**: May/Could
   **Explanation**: "Might I/May I/Could I" — polite request.

8. **fix_error** — "She mights come."
   **Hint**: modal no -s
   **Answer**: She might come.
   **Explanation**: Modals don't take -s. "She might" only.

9. **fix_error** — "He might to call."
   **Hint**: No "to" after might
   **Answer**: He might call.
   **Explanation**: After modal, base form without "to".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I might visit my parents this weekend. She might be late. They might call us tonight."
    **Question**: Who might be late?
    **Answer**: she
    **Explanation**: The text says "She might be late".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"It might rain."** — weather possibility
2. **"She might be late."** — possible state
3. **"We might go to the beach."** — uncertain plan
4. **"He might be home."** — possible location
5. **"I might visit my parents."** — possible action

## Unit I04 — It can't be true

> **Sub-CEFR**: B1 | **Grammar focus**: can't for impossibility (logical) | **Tense**: MODAL · can't
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: You express logical impossibility.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "That _____ be true!"
   **Options**: can't / mustn't / shouldn't
   **Answer**: can't
   **Explanation**: "Can't / Cannot" for logical impossibility — "It can't / cannot be true!" = it's impossible.

2. **multiple_choice** — "He _____ be there now — I just saw him here."
   **Options**: can't / shouldn't / mustn't
   **Answer**: can't
   **Explanation**: Logical impossibility.

3. **word_bank** — "It _____ be that easy."
   **Choices**: can't / shouldn't / wouldn't / wasn't
   **Answer**: can't
   **Explanation**: "Can't / Cannot be" — logical impossibility.

4. **word_bank** — "She _____ be 100 — she looks 30."
   **Choices**: can't / mustn't / wouldn't / shouldn't
   **Answer**: can't
   **Explanation**: Logical conclusion: impossible.

5. **fill_gap** — "That _____ be right — it's wrong."
   **Hint**: impossibility
   **Answer**: can't
   **Explanation**: "Can't / Cannot be right" — impossible.

6. **fill_gap** — "They _____ be coming — it's too late."
   **Hint**: impossibility (continuous)
   **Answer**: can't
   **Explanation**: "Can't / Cannot be coming" — impossibility with continuous.

7. **fill_gap** — "He _____ be at home — his car is here."
   **Hint**: logical conclusion
   **Answer**: can't
   **Explanation**: Logical: if car here, he's not home.

8. **fix_error** — "She mustn't be at home." (meaning impossible)
   **Hint**: For impossibility, use can't
   **Answer**: She can't be at home.
   **Explanation**: "Mustn't / Must not" = prohibition. "Can't / Cannot" = impossibility.

9. **fix_error** — "It can't to be true."
   **Hint**: No "to" after can't
   **Answer**: It can't be true.
   **Explanation**: Modal + base, no "to".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "That can't be John — he's in Paris. It can't be that simple. They can't be late — the meeting starts at 9."
    **Question**: Why is it not John?
    **Answer**: he's in Paris
    **Accepts**: in Paris
    **Explanation**: The text says "he's / he is in Paris" — reason it can't be him.

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

> **Sub-CEFR**: B1 | **Grammar focus**: Integration might/may/could/can't | **Tense**: MODAL · might/may/could

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)
