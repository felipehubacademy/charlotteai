# Module M08 — Who, which, that

> **Level**: Inter (B1)
> **Block**: B1 Block
> **Units**: 5 (I01–I05)
> **Theme**: Relative clauses defining (who, which, that, where, whose)
> **Module goal**: Student describes people, things, and places using defining relative clauses.
> **Connects to**: M09 (as...as comparisons).

## Module chunks introduced (~20)

- who / which / that / where / whose
- The person who...
- The book which/that...
- The place where...
- The woman whose...

## Unit I01 — The person who

> **Sub-CEFR**: B1 | **Grammar focus**: who for people in defining clauses | **Tense**: RELATIVE CLAUSE
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: You describe people by what they do.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "She's the woman _____ lives next door."
   **Options**: who / which / where
   **Answer**: who
   **Explanation**: "Who" for people in defining relative clauses.

2. **multiple_choice** — "He's the man _____ called yesterday."
   **Options**: who / which / where
   **Answer**: who
   **Explanation**: "Who" for people, subject.

3. **word_bank** — "The doctor _____ helps me is kind."
   **Choices**: who / which / where / whose
   **Answer**: who
   **Explanation**: People → "who".

4. **word_bank** — "I know a girl _____ speaks five languages."
   **Choices**: who / which / where / whose
   **Answer**: who
   **Explanation**: "A girl who speaks" — person.

5. **fill_gap** — "She's the friend _____ I told you about."
   **Hint**: relative pronoun for person
   **Answer**: who
   **Accepts**: that
   **Explanation**: "Who" or "that" for people.

6. **fill_gap** — "The teacher _____ teaches us is great."
   **Hint**: person, subject
   **Answer**: who
   **Explanation**: "Who teaches".

7. **fill_gap** — "He's the guy _____ won the race."
   **Hint**: person
   **Answer**: who
   **Explanation**: "Who won".

8. **fix_error** — "He's the man which called me."
   **Hint**: which is for things, not people
   **Answer**: He's the man who called me.
   **Explanation**: "Who" for people, "which" for things.

9. **fix_error** — "She's the girl, who lives here." (defining = no comma)
   **Hint**: No comma in defining clause
   **Answer**: She's the girl who lives here.
   **Explanation**: Defining clauses have NO commas. Identifies which girl.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "She's the woman who works with me. He's the man who called yesterday. They're the people who live next door."
    **Question**: Who works with the speaker?
    **Answer**: the woman
    **Explanation**: The text says "the woman who works with me".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"She's the woman who lives next door."** — defining who
2. **"He's the man who called."** — person + action
3. **"The doctor who helps me is kind."** — description
4. **"I know a girl who speaks five languages."** — describing ability
5. **"She's the friend I told you about."** — who omitted (informal)

## Unit I02 — The book which

> **Sub-CEFR**: B1 | **Grammar focus**: which/that for things in defining clauses | **Tense**: RELATIVE CLAUSE
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: You describe things by features.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "It's a book _____ changed my life."
   **Options**: which / who / where
   **Answer**: which
   **Explanation**: "Which" for things in defining clauses. "That" also works.

2. **multiple_choice** — "I have a phone _____ works underwater."
   **Options**: that / who / where
   **Answer**: that
   **Explanation**: "That" for things (or which).

3. **word_bank** — "She bought a car _____ runs on electricity."
   **Choices**: which / who / where / whose
   **Answer**: which
   **Explanation**: Thing → which/that.

4. **word_bank** — "This is the movie _____ won the Oscar."
   **Choices**: that / who / where / whose
   **Answer**: that
   **Explanation**: Movie = thing → that/which.

5. **fill_gap** — "I love books _____ teach me something."
   **Hint**: things
   **Answer**: that
   **Accepts**: which
   **Explanation**: "Books that teach" — thing + action.

6. **fill_gap** — "The phone _____ I bought is broken."
   **Hint**: thing, object of clause
   **Answer**: that
   **Accepts**: which, / omitted
   **Explanation**: "The phone that I bought" — object: that/which/omitted.

7. **fill_gap** — "It's the song _____ I love."
   **Hint**: thing
   **Answer**: that
   **Accepts**: which
   **Explanation**: "Song that I love".

8. **fix_error** — "The book who I read was great."
   **Hint**: who is for people
   **Answer**: The book that I read was great.
   **Explanation**: Book = thing → "that" or "which".

9. **fix_error** — "He has a car, which is red." (defining = no comma)
   **Hint**: No comma in defining
   **Answer**: He has a car which is red.
   **Accepts**: He has a red car
   **Explanation**: Defining → no comma. Non-defining (info adicional) → comma — but M19 covers that.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I have a phone which has a great camera. I love books that take me to other worlds."
    **Question**: What does the speaker love about books?
    **Answer**: take me to other worlds
    **Explanation**: The text says "books that take me to other worlds".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"It's a book which changed my life."** — which thing
2. **"I have a phone that works underwater."** — that thing
3. **"She bought a car which runs on electricity."** — descriptive
4. **"This is the movie that won the Oscar."** — defining
5. **"I love books that teach me something."** — general

## Unit I04 — Whose

> **Sub-CEFR**: B1 | **Grammar focus**: whose for possession | **Tense**: RELATIVE CLAUSE
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: You describe people by what they own or who's related.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "She's the woman _____ husband is a doctor."
   **Options**: whose / who / which
   **Answer**: whose
   **Explanation**: "Whose" for possession.

2. **multiple_choice** — "He's the guy _____ car broke down."
   **Options**: whose / who / which
   **Answer**: whose
   **Explanation**: "Whose car" — possession.

3. **word_bank** — "I know a man _____ daughter is famous."
   **Choices**: whose / who / which / where
   **Answer**: whose
   **Explanation**: Possession → whose.

4. **word_bank** — "She's the friend _____ birthday is today."
   **Choices**: whose / who / which / where
   **Answer**: whose
   **Explanation**: "Whose birthday".

5. **fill_gap** — "He's the teacher _____ class I love."
   **Hint**: possession
   **Answer**: whose
   **Explanation**: "Whose class" — possession.

6. **fill_gap** — "It's the dog _____ name I forgot."
   **Hint**: possession (animal)
   **Answer**: whose
   **Explanation**: "Whose name" — possession.

7. **fill_gap** — "She's the woman _____ kids are in my class."
   **Hint**: possession
   **Answer**: whose
   **Explanation**: "Whose kids".

8. **fix_error** — "He's the man who's car is red."
   **Hint**: "Who's" = "who is"; needs possessive
   **Answer**: He's the man whose car is red.
   **Explanation**: "Whose" (possessive) vs "who's" (who is). Common confusion.

9. **fix_error** — "She's the friend her name is Ana."
   **Hint**: Use whose for possession in relative clause
   **Answer**: She's the friend whose name is Ana.
   **Explanation**: "Whose" connects in relative clause: "the friend whose name".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "She's the woman whose husband is a doctor. He's the guy whose car I borrowed. I have a friend whose birthday is today."
    **Question**: Whose birthday is today?
    **Answer**: my friend's
    **Accepts**: a friend's
    **Explanation**: The text says "a friend whose birthday is today".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"She's the woman whose husband is a doctor."** — relation
2. **"He's the guy whose car broke down."** — possession
3. **"I know a man whose daughter is famous."** — relation
4. **"She's the friend whose birthday is today."** — event
5. **"He's the teacher whose class I love."** — possession

**Persona**: Charlotte
**Persona outfit**: `charlotte_casual`

**Opening line**: "Describe people using 'whose'."

**Sub-objectives** (3):

## Unit I05 — All relatives combined

> **Sub-CEFR**: B1 | **Grammar focus**: Integration who/which/that/where/whose | **Tense**: RELATIVE CLAUSE
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: You combine all relative pronouns in descriptions.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "She's the friend _____ I trust."
   **Options**: who / which / where
   **Answer**: who
   **Explanation**: Person → who.

2. **multiple_choice** — "It's a movie _____ I watched."
   **Options**: that / who / where
   **Answer**: that
   **Explanation**: Thing → that/which.

3. **word_bank** — "This is the city _____ I work."
   **Choices**: where / who / which / whose
   **Answer**: where
   **Explanation**: Place → where.

4. **word_bank** — "She's the woman _____ son is my classmate."
   **Choices**: whose / who / which / where
   **Answer**: whose
   **Explanation**: Possession → whose.

5. **fill_gap** — "He's the doctor _____ helped me."
   **Hint**: person
   **Answer**: who
   **Explanation**: Who for people.

6. **fill_gap** — "I have a phone _____ takes great photos."
   **Hint**: thing
   **Answer**: that
   **Accepts**: which
   **Explanation**: That/which for things.

7. **fill_gap** — "The school _____ I studied is famous."
   **Hint**: place
   **Answer**: where
   **Explanation**: Where for places.

8. **fix_error** — "He's the man which I met."
   **Hint**: person needs who
   **Answer**: He's the man who I met.
   **Explanation**: Who for people.

9. **fix_error** — "The book who I read."
   **Hint**: thing needs that/which
   **Answer**: The book that I read.
   **Explanation**: Book = thing → that.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "She's the friend who I trust. It's the city where I work. He's the man whose son is famous. This is the movie that won."
    **Question**: What won?
    **Answer**: the movie
    **Explanation**: The text says "the movie that won".
