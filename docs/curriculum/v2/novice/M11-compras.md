# Module M11 — Compras

> **Level**: Novice (A1)
> **Block**: A1 Block (fechamento)
> **Units**: 5 (N01–N05)
> **Theme**: Lojas, preços, pedidos, roupas básicas
> **Module goal**: Aluno sai sabendo navegar em lojas usando "I'd like", demonstratives (this/that/these/those), preposições básicas de lugar, e vocabulário de roupas e preços.
> **Connects to**: M12 (Comparações) — depois de comparar objetos numa loja, aprende comparativos formais (-er, more than).

## Module chunks introduced (~35)

- I'd like to buy + item
- I'm looking for + item
- this / that / these / those
- in / on / at / next to / between / behind / in front of
- shop / store / supermarket / shopping mall
- price / cheap / expensive
- shirt / pants / dress / shoes / jacket / hat
- size / fit / try on
- credit card / cash
- Can I pay with...?
- It's on sale.
- Where is the...?

## Unit N01 — Na loja

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: I'd like + noun + prepositions of place | **Tense**: EXPRESSÕES
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você entra numa loja procurando algo específico.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "_____ to buy a shirt."
   **Options**: I'd like / I want / I have
   **Answer**: I'd like
   **Explanation**: "I'd / I would like to + verb" é o chunk educado pra falar do que quer. "I want" é direto demais em loja.

2. **multiple_choice** — "I'm looking _____ a black shirt."
   **Options**: for / at / to
   **Answer**: for
   **Explanation**: "Look for" = procurar. Chunk fixo: "I'm / I am looking FOR + objeto". Não confundir com "look at" (olhar pra).

3. **word_bank** — "The shirts are _____ the second floor."
   **Choices**: on / in / at / next
   **Answer**: on
   **Explanation**: Andares usam "on": "on the second floor", "on the third floor". Lugares específicos como hotéis.

4. **word_bank** — "_____ store opens at nine."
   **Choices**: The / A / An / One
   **Answer**: The
   **Explanation**: "The store" — refere-se a loja específica em contexto. "The" pra coisa já conhecida.

5. **fill_gap** — "I'd like to _____ this dress, please — to see how it fits."
   **Hint**: Verbo "experimentar" roupa
   **Answer**: try on
   **Explanation**: "Try on" = experimentar (roupa). Phrasal verb fixo: "Can I try on this dress?".

6. **fill_gap** — "The cashier is _____ the door."
   **Hint**: Preposição "perto de"
   **Answer**: next to
   **Explanation**: "Next to" = ao lado de. Localização: "next to the door".

7. **fill_gap** — "I'd like _____ buy two shirts."
   **Hint**: Particula entre "like" e o verbo
   **Answer**: to
   **Explanation**: "I'd / I would like TO + verbo base": "I'd / I would like to buy". Após "like", "to + base".

8. **fix_error** — "I want one shirt black."
   **Hint**: Ordem do adjetivo + educado
   **Answer**: I'd like a black shirt.
   **Explanation**: Adjetivo VEM ANTES do substantivo: "a BLACK shirt" (não "a shirt black"). E "I'd / I would like" é mais educado que "I want".

9. **fix_error** — "I'm looking the menu."
   **Hint**: Preposição correta com "look"
   **Answer**: I'm looking at the menu.
   **Explanation**: "Look at" = olhar para algo. "Look for" = procurar. Aqui o sentido é olhar, então "look at the menu".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I'd like to buy a black jacket. I'm looking for size M. Excuse me, where is the cashier? It's next to the door."
    **Question**: Onde fica o caixa?
    **Answer**: next to the door
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "It's / It is next to the door".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I'd like to buy a shirt."** — polite request
2. **"I'm looking for size M."** — looking for chunk
3. **"Where is the cashier?"** — location question
4. **"The shirts are on the second floor."** — preposition on + floor
5. **"Can I try on this dress?"** — try on

## Unit N02 — Este, esse, aquele

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: Demonstratives this / that / these / those | **Tense**: EXPRESSÕES
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você aponta para objetos próximos (this/these) ou distantes (that/those) na loja.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "_____ shirt is nice (in my hand)."
   **Options**: This / That / These / Those
   **Answer**: This
   **Explanation**: "This" → objeto SINGULAR e PRÓXIMO. "Esta camisa (que estou segurando)".

2. **multiple_choice** — "_____ dress over there is beautiful."
   **Options**: That / This / These / Those
   **Answer**: That
   **Explanation**: "That" → objeto SINGULAR e DISTANTE. "Aquele vestido (lá longe)".

3. **word_bank** — "_____ shoes are too big."
   **Choices**: These / This / That / Those
   **Answer**: These
   **Explanation**: "These" → plural PRÓXIMO. "Estes sapatos (nos meus pés)".

4. **word_bank** — "_____ pants on the shelf are nice."
   **Choices**: Those / This / These / That
   **Answer**: Those
   **Explanation**: "Those" → plural DISTANTE. "Aquelas calças (na prateleira lá)".

5. **fill_gap** — "I'd like to try _____ shirt on, please. (close)"
   **Hint**: Singular próximo
   **Answer**: this
   **Explanation**: "This shirt" pra um próximo. Singular + perto.

6. **fill_gap** — "How much are _____ shoes? (close, on my feet)"
   **Hint**: Plural próximo
   **Answer**: these
   **Explanation**: "These shoes" — plural perto. "Estes sapatos".

7. **fill_gap** — "Look at _____ jackets over there!"
   **Hint**: Plural distante
   **Answer**: those
   **Explanation**: "Those jackets over there" — plural distante. "Aquelas jaquetas lá".

8. **fix_error** — "I like this shoes."
   **Hint**: Shoes é plural — qual demonstrativo?
   **Answer**: I like these shoes.
   **Explanation**: "Shoes" é plural → "these" (perto) ou "those" (longe). "This" só pra singular.

9. **fix_error** — "Those is my shirt."
   **Hint**: Concordância plural-singular
   **Answer**: That is my shirt.
   **Explanation**: "Shirt" singular → "That" (não "Those"). Pra plural seria "Those are my shirts".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I like this dress (in my hand). That dress over there is too expensive. These shoes fit me. Those shoes don't fit."
    **Question**: Qual item é caro?
    **Answer**: that dress
    **Accepts**: the dress over there
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "That dress over there is too expensive".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"This shirt is nice."** — this singular close
2. **"That dress is beautiful."** — that singular far
3. **"These shoes fit me."** — these plural close
4. **"Those pants are expensive."** — those plural far
5. **"I'd like this one, please."** — this + one

## Unit N03 — Em cima, embaixo, ao lado

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: Prepositions of place (in, on, at, next to, between, behind, in front of) | **Tense**: EXPRESSÕES
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você diz onde estão as coisas — em prateleiras, ao lado de outras, atrás do balcão.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "The cookies are _____ the shelf."
   **Options**: on / in / at
   **Answer**: on
   **Explanation**: "On" → superfície horizontal. Prateleira é superfície → "on the shelf".

2. **multiple_choice** — "My keys are _____ my bag."
   **Options**: in / on / at
   **Answer**: in
   **Explanation**: "In" → DENTRO. Bag tem interior → "in my bag".

3. **word_bank** — "I'm _____ the store now."
   **Choices**: at / on / in / next
   **Answer**: at
   **Explanation**: "At + lugar específico/ponto": at the store, at home, at school. Localização geral.

4. **word_bank** — "The bank is _____ the post office and the cafe."
   **Choices**: between / in / on / behind
   **Answer**: between
   **Explanation**: "Between" → entre DUAS coisas. "Between A and B".

5. **fill_gap** — "The cashier is _____ the door."
   **Hint**: Preposição "perto de / ao lado"
   **Answer**: next to
   **Explanation**: "Next to" = ao lado de. "The cashier is next to the door".

6. **fill_gap** — "My shoes are _____ the box."
   **Hint**: Preposição "atrás de"
   **Answer**: behind
   **Explanation**: "Behind" = atrás de. "Behind the box".

7. **fill_gap** — "The car is _____ the building."
   **Hint**: Preposição "em frente a"
   **Answer**: in front of
   **Explanation**: "In front of" = em frente a. Três palavras como uma só.

8. **fix_error** — "I'm in the bus stop."
   **Hint**: Stop é lugar específico
   **Answer**: I'm at the bus stop.
   **Explanation**: "Bus stop" é lugar específico/ponto → "at". "In" significaria dentro do ponto (não físico).

9. **fix_error** — "My phone is on my bag."
   **Hint**: Bag tem interior
   **Answer**: My phone is in my bag.
   **Explanation**: Bag tem dentro → "in" (não "on"). "On the bag" seria em cima da bolsa por fora.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I'm at the supermarket. The bread is on the second shelf. The milk is in the fridge. The cookies are next to the cash register."
    **Question**: Onde estão os biscoitos?
    **Answer**: next to the cash register
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "next to the cash register".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"The cookies are on the shelf."** — on + surface
2. **"My keys are in my bag."** — in + container
3. **"I'm at the store."** — at + location
4. **"The cashier is next to the door."** — next to
5. **"The car is in front of the building."** — in front of

## Unit N04 — Pagando

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: Payment chunks + money vocab | **Tense**: EXPRESSÕES
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você está no caixa pagando uma compra — cartão, dinheiro, troco.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "Can I pay _____ credit card?"
   **Options**: with / by / for
   **Answer**: with
   **Explanation**: "Pay with" + método. "Pay with cash", "pay with credit card". Note: "by card" também existe mas "with" é mais comum em diálogo.

2. **multiple_choice** — "_____ is the total?"
   **Options**: How much / How many / What much
   **Answer**: How much
   **Explanation**: Preço/total sempre "How much": "How much is the total?".

3. **word_bank** — "It's _____ sale today."
   **Choices**: on / in / at / for
   **Answer**: on
   **Explanation**: "On sale" = em promoção. Chunk fixo: "It's / It is on sale".

4. **word_bank** — "I'd like to pay _____ cash."
   **Choices**: with / by / for / in
   **Answer**: with
   **Explanation**: "Pay with cash" / "pay in cash" — ambas funcionam. "With" é mais comum em conversação.

5. **fill_gap** — "Your total is twenty _____."
   **Hint**: Moeda americana
   **Answer**: dollars
   **Explanation**: "Twenty dollars" — preço em USD. "Bucks" é gíria.

6. **fill_gap** — "Can I have a _____, please?"
   **Hint**: Comprovante de compra
   **Answer**: receipt
   **Explanation**: "Receipt" = comprovante/recibo. "Can I have a receipt?" — pedido padrão após pagar.

7. **fill_gap** — "_____ shirt is twenty dollars."
   **Hint**: Demonstrativo singular próximo
   **Answer**: This
   **Explanation**: "This shirt is twenty dollars" — pode pegar e mostrar a peça.

8. **fix_error** — "How much it is?"
   **Hint**: Ordem da pergunta
   **Answer**: How much is it?
   **Explanation**: WH + verbo + sujeito: "How much IS it?", não "How much it is?". Inversão correta.

9. **fix_error** — "I pay for cash."
   **Hint**: Preposição com cash
   **Answer**: I pay with cash.
   **Explanation**: "Pay with cash" — preposição é "with" (ou "in"). "For" seria "pagar POR algo (item)".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "Customer: How much is this jacket? Cashier: Forty dollars. Customer: Can I pay with credit card? Cashier: Of course. Here's your receipt."
    **Question**: Como o cliente paga?
    **Answer**: with credit card
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "Can I pay with credit card?".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"How much is it?"** — price question
2. **"Can I pay with credit card?"** — payment method
3. **"It's on sale today."** — sale chunk
4. **"Can I have a receipt?"** — receipt request
5. **"Your total is fifty dollars."** — total announcement

**Voiced by**: `charlotte`
**Persona**: Charlotte
**Persona outfit**: `charlotte_casual`
**Time budget**: 90s
**Opening line**: "Hi! Ready to check out?"

1. **id**: 1
   **label_pt**: "Perguntar o total"

## Unit N05 — Comprando completo

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: Integration M11 (I'd like + demonstratives + prepositions + payment) | **Tense**: EXPRESSÕES
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você completa uma compra do início ao fim numa loja de roupas.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "_____ to buy this jacket."
   **Options**: I'd like / I want / I do
   **Answer**: I'd like
   **Explanation**: "I'd / I would like to buy" — chunk educado pra anunciar intenção de compra.

2. **multiple_choice** — "_____ shoes over there are nice."
   **Options**: Those / This / These / That
   **Answer**: Those
   **Explanation**: "Shoes" plural + "over there" (distante) → "Those".

3. **word_bank** — "The cashier is _____ the door."
   **Choices**: next to / in / on / at
   **Answer**: next to
   **Explanation**: "Next to the door" = ao lado da porta.

4. **word_bank** — "Can I pay _____ credit card?"
   **Choices**: with / by / on / for
   **Answer**: with
   **Explanation**: "Pay with + method" — preposição padrão.

5. **fill_gap** — "I'd like to _____ this dress on, please — in the fitting room."
   **Hint**: Experimentar roupa
   **Answer**: try
   **Explanation**: "Try on" = experimentar. "I'd / I would like to try this dress on" ou "try on this dress".

6. **fill_gap** — "The shirts are _____ the second floor."
   **Hint**: Preposição com andar
   **Answer**: on
   **Explanation**: "On the second floor" — andares usam "on".

7. **fill_gap** — "_____ is the total?"
   **Hint**: Pergunta de preço
   **Answer**: How much
   **Explanation**: "How much is the total?" — pergunta de valor total.

8. **fix_error** — "I want this shoes."
   **Hint**: Demonstrativo + plural
   **Answer**: I'd like these shoes.
   **Explanation**: "Shoes" plural exige "these" (perto) ou "those" (longe). E "I'd / I would like" mais educado.

9. **fix_error** — "I pay for cash."
   **Hint**: Preposição com cash
   **Answer**: I pay with cash.
   **Explanation**: "Pay with cash" — preposição "with". "For" seria "pagar POR algo".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I'd like to buy this shirt. How much is it? Twenty dollars. Can I try it on? Yes. The fitting room is next to the cashier."
    **Question**: Quanto custa a camisa?
    **Answer**: twenty dollars
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "Twenty dollars".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

## Cross-unit consolidation

Ao terminar M11, o aluno deve usar naturalmente:
- "I'd like to + verb" / "I'm looking for + item" em qualquer loja
- this/that/these/those por proximidade e número
- Prepositions of place: in/on/at/next to/between/behind/in front of
- "How much is it?" / "Can I pay with...?" / "Can I have a receipt?"
- "Try on" pra roupas, "on sale" pra promoções

Esses chunks fecham o Bloco A1. Próximo: M12 (Comparações) abre o Bloco A2 com -er/more than.
