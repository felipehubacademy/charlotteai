# Module M09 — Agora mesmo

> **Level**: Novice (A1)
> **Block**: A1 Block
> **Units**: 5 (N01–N05)
> **Theme**: O que está acontecendo neste momento — present continuous
> **Module goal**: Aluno sai sabendo formar e usar present continuous (I'm doing, he's working), distinguir de present simple (rotina vs agora), e descrever cenas presentes.
> **Connects to**: M10 (Comida e bebida) — depois de dominar continuous, aplica em situações como "I'm eating", "She's drinking".

## Module chunks introduced (~30)

- I am + verb-ing / I'm + verb-ing
- you are + verb-ing / you're + verb-ing
- he is + verb-ing / he's + verb-ing
- she is + verb-ing / she's + verb-ing
- we are + verb-ing / we're + verb-ing
- they are + verb-ing / they're + verb-ing
- I'm not working / He isn't studying
- Are you working? / Is she sleeping?
- What are you doing? / What is he doing?
- right now / at the moment / today
- running, sitting, swimming (spelling -ing rules)
- contrast: I work in NYC (rotina) vs I'm working today (agora)

## Unit N01 — Estou fazendo agora

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: Present continuous positivo (am/is/are + verb-ing)
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você descreve o que está fazendo agora — durante uma chamada de vídeo, por exemplo.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ working right now."
   **Options**: am / is / are
   **Answer**: am
   **Explanation**: Com "I" usa "am" no present continuous: "I am working" ou contraído "I'm working".

2. **multiple_choice** — "She _____ sleeping."
   **Options**: is / am / are
   **Answer**: is
   **Explanation**: Com he/she/it usa "is": "She is sleeping" ou "She's sleeping".

3. **word_bank** — "They _____ playing soccer."
   **Choices**: are / is / am / be
   **Answer**: are
   **Explanation**: Com they/we/you usa "are": "They are playing" ou "They're playing".

4. **word_bank** — "I'm _____ a book."
   **Choices**: reading / read / readed / readinge
   **Answer**: reading
   **Explanation**: Present continuous: to be + verb-ing. "Read" → "reading" (apenas adiciona -ing).

5. **fill_gap** — "He _____ watching TV."
   **Hint**: Verbo "to be" 3ª pessoa
   **Answer**: is
   **Explanation**: "He is watching" — present continuous com he/she/it usa "is".

6. **fill_gap** — "We are _____ in the park."
   **Hint**: Verbo "correr" no -ing (dobra o n)
   **Answer**: running
   **Explanation**: Verbos curtos terminados em consoante-vogal-consoante dobram a última consoante antes de -ing: run → running, sit → sitting, swim → swimming.

7. **fill_gap** — "She is _____ a cake."
   **Hint**: Verbo "fazer" no -ing (drop o -e)
   **Answer**: making
   **Explanation**: Verbos terminados em -e mudo perdem o e antes de -ing: make → making, write → writing, take → taking.

8. **fix_error** — "I am work right now."
   **Hint**: Falta -ing
   **Answer**: I am working right now.
   **Explanation**: Present continuous = to be + verb-ING. "I am working", não "I am work".

9. **fix_error** — "She working."
   **Hint**: Falta verbo "to be"
   **Answer**: She is working.
   **Explanation**: Present continuous SEMPRE precisa de "to be" + verb-ing. "She is working" ou "She's working", nunca "She working".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I'm working from home today. My sister is studying upstairs. My mother is cooking lunch. We are all busy!"
    **Question**: What is the sister doing?
    **Answer**: studying
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "My sister is studying upstairs".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I'm working right now."** — own action now
2. **"She is sleeping."** — 3rd person
3. **"They are playing soccer."** — plural
4. **"We are eating dinner."** — group activity
5. **"He's reading a book."** — contraction he's

## Unit N02 — Não estou fazendo

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: Present continuous negativo (am not / isn't / aren't + verb-ing)
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você corrige uma suposição errada ou diz o que NÃO está acontecendo agora.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ working today. It's Sunday."
   **Options**: am not / is not / don't
   **Answer**: am not
   **Explanation**: Negativa com "I": "I am not working" ou "I'm not working". Não usa don't (que é present simple).

2. **multiple_choice** — "She _____ sleeping; she's reading."
   **Options**: isn't / aren't / doesn't
   **Answer**: isn't
   **Explanation**: "She isn't sleeping" — 3ª pessoa negativa = is + not = isn't. Forma contraída padrão.

3. **word_bank** — "We _____ eating; we're talking."
   **Choices**: aren't / isn't / am not / don't
   **Answer**: aren't
   **Explanation**: "We aren't" — plural negativa = are + not = aren't.

4. **word_bank** — "He _____ working today; he's sick."
   **Choices**: isn't / aren't / doesn't / no
   **Answer**: isn't
   **Explanation**: "He isn't working" — 3ª pessoa singular negativa.

5. **fill_gap** — "I _____ studying right now."
   **Hint**: Negativa com "I" (forma contraída)
   **Answer**: 'm not
   **Explanation**: "I'm not studying" — "am not" contrai pra "'m not". Não existe "amn't" em inglês padrão.

6. **fill_gap** — "She _____ cooking."
   **Hint**: Negativa de "she is" (forma contraída)
   **Answer**: isn't
   **Explanation**: "She isn't cooking" — is + not contrai pra "isn't".

7. **fill_gap** — "They _____ watching TV; they're playing games."
   **Hint**: Negativa de "they are" (contraída)
   **Answer**: aren't
   **Explanation**: "They aren't watching" — are + not = aren't.

8. **fix_error** — "She don't working."
   **Hint**: Present continuous negativa usa "isn't"
   **Answer**: She isn't working.
   **Explanation**: Present continuous negativa = forma de "to be" + not + verb-ing. NÃO usa don't/doesn't (que é present simple). "She isn't working".

9. **fix_error** — "I amn't sleeping."
   **Hint**: "Amn't" não existe em inglês padrão
   **Answer**: I'm not sleeping.
   **Explanation**: "Amn't" NÃO existe — apenas "I'm not". A contração de "am not" é só "'m not".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I'm not working today. My brother isn't studying — he's playing games. We aren't eating yet."
    **Question**: What isn't the brother doing?
    **Answer**: studying
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "My brother isn't studying".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I'm not working today."** — own negative
2. **"She isn't sleeping."** — 3rd person negative
3. **"They aren't eating."** — plural negative
4. **"He isn't working today."** — temporary contrast
5. **"We aren't watching TV."** — group negative

## Unit N03 — Está fazendo?

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: Present continuous questions (Are you...? Is she...?)
> **Markers**: [qform]
> **Real-life context**: Você liga pra alguém e pergunta o que essa pessoa está fazendo.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "_____ you working right now?"
   **Options**: Are / Is / Do
   **Answer**: Are
   **Explanation**: Pergunta de present continuous: inverte "to be". "Are you working?" — "you" usa "are".

2. **multiple_choice** — "_____ she sleeping?"
   **Options**: Is / Are / Does
   **Answer**: Is
   **Explanation**: "Is she sleeping?" — he/she/it usa "is" invertido na pergunta.

3. **word_bank** — "_____ they playing soccer?"
   **Choices**: Are / Is / Do / Does
   **Answer**: Are
   **Explanation**: "Are they playing?" — they/we/you usam "are".

4. **word_bank** — "What _____ you doing?"
   **Choices**: are / is / do / does
   **Answer**: are
   **Explanation**: WH-question com you: "What are you doing?" — "are" após o WH.

5. **fill_gap** — "_____ he watching TV?"
   **Hint**: To be 3ª pessoa em pergunta
   **Answer**: Is
   **Explanation**: "Is he watching?" — "he" usa "is" invertido.

6. **fill_gap** — "What _____ she doing now?"
   **Hint**: To be 3ª pessoa
   **Answer**: is
   **Explanation**: "What is she doing?" — WH + is + sujeito + verb-ing.

7. **fill_gap** — "Yes, I _____." (responda a "Are you working?")
   **Hint**: Resposta curta afirmativa
   **Answer**: am
   **Explanation**: "Yes, I am" — short answer com to be. Repete o auxiliar.

8. **fix_error** — "Do you working?"
   **Hint**: Present continuous não usa "do"
   **Answer**: Are you working?
   **Explanation**: Continuous pergunta com "to be" invertido, não com "do/does". "Are you working?", não "Do you working?".

9. **fix_error** — "Is she works?"
   **Hint**: Continuous precisa de verb-ing
   **Answer**: Is she working?
   **Explanation**: Após "Is + sujeito", verb-ing: "Is she working?". "Works" seria present simple (sem -ing).

2. **"Is she sleeping?"** — question about her
3. **"What are you doing?"** — common WH-question
4. **"Yes, I am. No, I'm not."** — short answers

## Unit N04 — Hoje, agora, neste momento

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: Time expressions for continuous (right now / at the moment / today / this week)
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você descreve atividades temporárias — desta semana, deste mês, neste momento.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I'm working _____."
   **Options**: right now / every day / on Mondays
   **Answer**: right now
   **Explanation**: "Right now" combina com continuous (ação atual). "Every day" e "on Mondays" combinam com present simple (rotina).

2. **multiple_choice** — "She's studying English _____."
   **Options**: this week / every week / on Wednesdays
   **Answer**: this week
   **Explanation**: "This week" indica atividade temporária — combina com continuous. "Every week" é rotina (simple).

3. **word_bank** — "He's working from home _____."
   **Choices**: today / on weekdays / never / once
   **Answer**: today
   **Explanation**: "Today" pode indicar temporário (não como sempre) — combina com continuous.

4. **word_bank** — "We're eating outside _____."
   **Choices**: at the moment / never / always / on Sundays
   **Answer**: at the moment
   **Explanation**: "At the moment" = agora mesmo. Sinaliza continuous.

5. **fill_gap** — "I'm reading a great book _____."
   **Hint**: Marcador temporal pra agora
   **Answer**: right now
   **Explanation**: "Right now" enfatiza a ação no momento. Continuous + right now é combinação natural.

6. **fill_gap** — "She is staying with us _____."
   **Hint**: Marcador de período curto (semana atual)
   **Answer**: this week
   **Explanation**: "This week" — período presente e específico. Combina com continuous.

7. **fill_gap** — "They're traveling _____."
   **Hint**: Marcador de período atual (mês)
   **Answer**: this month
   **Explanation**: "This month" — atividade temporária em curso.

8. **fix_error** — "I'm working every day right now."
   **Hint**: "Every day" indica rotina, não momento
   **Answer**: I'm working a lot right now.
   **Explanation**: "Every day" + present continuous é contradição. Use "every day" com simple, ou "right now / today" com continuous. Aqui: troca pra "a lot right now".

9. **fix_error** — "She is studying on Mondays."
   **Hint**: "On Mondays" pede present simple
   **Answer**: She studies on Mondays.
   **Explanation**: "On Mondays" indica rotina — use present simple. Pra continuous, marcadores como "right now" ou "this week".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I'm working from home this week. My sister is visiting from London. We're having dinner together every night."
    **Question**: Where is the sister visiting from?
    **Answer**: London
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "My sister is visiting from London".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I'm working right now."** — right now
2. **"She's studying this week."** — this week
3. **"He's traveling at the moment."** — at the moment
4. **"We're eating outside today."** — today
5. **"They're staying with us this month."** — this month

## Unit N05 — Simple vs Continuous

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: Contrast present simple vs present continuous
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você diferencia o que faz NORMALMENTE (rotina) do que está fazendo AGORA (temporário).

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ in São Paulo (it's my home)."
   **Options**: live / am living / lives
   **Answer**: live
   **Explanation**: Lugar onde mora habitualmente (rotina) usa present simple: "I live". Continuous seria temporário.

2. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ in NYC this month (temporary stay)."
   **Options**: am living / live / lives
   **Answer**: am living
   **Explanation**: Estada temporária usa present continuous: "I am living in NYC this month". "Live" seria permanente.

3. **word_bank** — "She usually _____ coffee in the morning."
   **Choices**: drinks / is drinking / drink / drank
   **Answer**: drinks
   **Explanation**: "Usually" indica rotina → present simple. "She drinks coffee" — 3ª pessoa singular com -s.

4. **word_bank** — "We _____ dinner right now."
   **Choices**: are having / have / has / had
   **Answer**: are having
   **Explanation**: "Right now" sinaliza continuous: "We are having dinner". Present simple seria rotina diária.

5. **fill_gap** — "He _____ to the gym every day."
   **Hint**: rotina, 3ª pessoa
   **Answer**: goes
   **Explanation**: "Every day" indica rotina → present simple. "He goes to the gym" — 3ª pessoa singular, "go" vira "goes".

6. **fill_gap** — "She _____ Italian — she's a translator."
   **Hint**: Profissão / habilidade permanente
   **Answer**: speaks
   **Explanation**: Habilidade permanente usa present simple: "She speaks Italian". Não continuous.

7. **fill_gap** — "Look! It _____."
   **Hint**: Continuous (acontecendo agora)
   **Answer**: is raining
   **Explanation**: "Look! It is raining" — ação que está acontecendo neste momento. Continuous.

8. **fix_error** — "I am working in a bank every day."
   **Hint**: "Every day" pede present simple
   **Answer**: I work in a bank every day.
   **Explanation**: "Every day" indica rotina — use present simple: "I work". Continuous + every day é contradição.

9. **fix_error** — "She drinks coffee right now."
   **Hint**: "Right now" pede continuous
   **Answer**: She is drinking coffee right now.
   **Explanation**: "Right now" indica ação no momento — use continuous: "She is drinking". Present simple seria rotina.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I usually wake up at seven, but today I'm waking up at nine — it's Sunday! My brother always cooks on Sundays. He's making pancakes right now."
    **Question**: What is the brother making right now?
    **Answer**: pancakes
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "He's making pancakes right now".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I work in NYC."** — rotina permanente (simple)
2. **"I'm working from home today."** — temporário (continuous)
3. **"She speaks three languages."** — habilidade (simple)
4. **"She is speaking on the phone now."** — ação atual (continuous)
5. **"They usually play soccer, but today they're playing tennis."** — contrast

## Cross-unit consolidation

Ao terminar M09, o aluno deve usar naturalmente:
- Present continuous positivo: to be + verb-ing (I'm working, she's reading)
- Present continuous negativo: am not / isn't / aren't + verb-ing
- Present continuous questions: inversão de "to be" (Are you working? What is she doing?)
- Spelling -ing: running (dobra), making (drop e), studying (regular)
- Marcadores temporais: right now / at the moment / today / this week (combinam com continuous)
- Marcadores de rotina: every day / always / on Mondays (combinam com simple)
- Contraste claro: "I usually X, but I'm Ying right now"

Esses chunks abrem M10 (Comida e bebida), onde o aluno usa continuous em contextos reais — restaurante, cozinhar, beber.
