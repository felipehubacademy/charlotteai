# Module M17 — Não foi assim

> **Level**: Novice (A2)
> **Block**: A2 Block
> **Units**: 5 (N01–N05)
> **Theme**: Past simple negativo e perguntas (didn't / Did you...?)
> **Module goal**: Aluno sai sabendo formar negativas com didn't + base e perguntas com Did + base (regular e irregular).
> **Connects to**: M18 (Planos pra amanhã) — depois do passado completo, vai pro futuro com going to.

## Module chunks introduced (~25)

- I didn't / you didn't / she didn't / they didn't
- didn't + verbo base (não didn't worked)
- Did I...? / Did you...? / Did she...? / Did they...?
- Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
- Did + sujeito + verbo base + complemento?
- WH + did + sujeito + verbo base (Where did you go? What did she do?)

## Unit N01 — Eu não fiz

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Past simple negative (didn't + base) | **Tense**: PAST · simple · negative
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você esclarece o que NÃO fez ontem.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ work yesterday."
   **Options**: didn't / wasn't / haven't
   **Answer**: didn't
   **Explanation**: Past simple negativo: "didn't / did not + verbo base". "Didn't / Did not work" = não trabalhei.

2. **multiple_choice** — "She _____ go to the party."
   **Options**: didn't / wasn't / doesn't
   **Answer**: didn't
   **Explanation**: "Didn't / Did not" pra todas as pessoas no passado. "She didn't / did not go".

3. **word_bank** — "We _____ eat dinner."
   **Choices**: didn't / weren't / haven't / don't
   **Answer**: didn't
   **Explanation**: "Didn't / Did not" + base. Plural ou singular, todos usam didn't.

4. **word_bank** — "He _____ see the movie."
   **Choices**: didn't / wasn't / hadn't / doesn't
   **Answer**: didn't
   **Explanation**: "He didn't / did not see" — past negativo com didn't + base. Mesmo em 3ª pessoa, didn't (não doesn't).

5. **fill_gap** — "I _____ have time yesterday."
   **Hint**: Negativa de "I had"
   **Answer**: didn't
   **Explanation**: "I didn't / did not have" — passado negativo. Note: verbo base "have", não "had".

6. **fill_gap** — "She _____ call me."
   **Hint**: Negativa passado de "she called"
   **Answer**: didn't
   **Explanation**: "She didn't / did not call" — didn't + base "call" (não "called").

7. **fill_gap** — "They _____ go to school."
   **Hint**: Negativa passado plural
   **Answer**: didn't
   **Explanation**: "They didn't / did not go" — plural também usa didn't.

8. **fix_error** — "He didn't went home."
   **Hint**: Após didn't, verbo base
   **Answer**: He didn't go home.
   **Explanation**: Após "didn't / did not", verbo SEMPRE base: "go", não "went". "Didn't already carries past — verbo principal volta pra forma base.

9. **fix_error** — "She doesn't call me yesterday."
   **Hint**: Yesterday é passado, usa didn't
   **Answer**: She didn't call me yesterday.
   **Explanation**: "Yesterday" indica passado → "didn't / did not" (não "doesn't / does not" que é presente).

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I didn't work yesterday. I didn't go out either. I didn't see anyone. It was a quiet day."
    **Question**: O narrador trabalhou?
    **Answer**: no
    **Accepts**: didn't work
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "I didn't / did not work yesterday".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I didn't work yesterday."** — own negative past
2. **"She didn't go to the party."** — 3rd person negative
3. **"We didn't eat dinner."** — plural negative
4. **"He didn't see the movie."** — negative
5. **"They didn't call me."** — plural negative

## Unit N02 — Você fez?

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Past simple yes/no questions (Did you...?) | **Tense**: PAST · simple · question
> **Markers**: [denso, qform]

## Unit N03 — Onde, quando, o quê?

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Past WH-questions (Where did you...? What did she...?) | **Tense**: PAST · simple · question
> **Markers**: [qform]
> **Real-life context**: Você faz entrevistas curtas — onde, quando, com quem, por quê.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "Where _____ you go?"
   **Options**: did / do / were
   **Answer**: did
   **Explanation**: WH + did + sujeito + base: "Where did you go?".

2. **multiple_choice** — "What _____ she eat?"
   **Options**: did / does / was
   **Answer**: did
   **Explanation**: "What did she eat?" — past WH question com Did.

3. **word_bank** — "When _____ they arrive?"
   **Choices**: did / do / were / have
   **Answer**: did
   **Explanation**: "When did they arrive?" — pergunta WH no passado.

   **Choices**: did / was / does / had
   **Answer**: did
   **Explanation**: "Why did he leave?" — WH no passado + base.

   **Hint**: Auxiliar past WH

## Unit N04 — Eu nunca fiz isso

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Past simple negative — extension (never + did/didn't) | **Tense**: PAST · simple · negative
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você fala sobre o que nunca fez ou não fez.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ visited Japan."
   **Options**: never / didn't / not
   **Answer**: never
   **Explanation**: "I never visited" = "Nunca visitei". "Never" sozinho (sem didn't): "I never visited Japan".

2. **multiple_choice** — "She _____ come to the party."
   **Options**: didn't / not / no
   **Answer**: didn't
   **Explanation**: Past negativo padrão: "didn't / did not + base". "She didn't / did not come".

3. **word_bank** — "We _____ knew."
   **Choices**: never / didn't / no / not
   **Answer**: never
   **Explanation**: "We never knew" = "Nunca soubemos". Sem auxiliar — verbo no passado direto.

4. **word_bank** — "He _____ understand the question."
   **Choices**: didn't / never / not / no
   **Answer**: didn't
   **Explanation**: "He didn't / did not understand" — past negativo. (Could also be "never understood" but with simple/no auxiliary structure).

5. **fill_gap** — "I _____ travel abroad."
   **Hint**: Negativa direta de past
   **Answer**: didn't
   **Explanation**: "I didn't / did not travel" — padrão. "I never traveled" também funciona (sem didn't).

6. **fill_gap** — "She _____ said anything wrong."
   **Hint**: "Nunca" sem didn't
   **Answer**: never
   **Explanation**: "She never said" — passado direto sem auxiliar. Equivale a "She didn't / did not say anything".

7. **fill_gap** — "They _____ arrive on time."
   **Hint**: Past negativo padrão
   **Answer**: didn't
   **Explanation**: "They didn't / did not arrive" — passado negativo.

8. **fix_error** — "I never didn't go there."
   **Hint**: Dupla negativa
   **Answer**: I never went there.
   **Explanation**: NÃO usa dupla negativa em inglês. Ou "I never went" OU "I didn't / did not go" — não ambos.

9. **fix_error** — "She never come yesterday."
   **Hint**: Com "never", o verbo no passado simples
   **Answer**: She never came yesterday.
   **Explanation**: "Never" + verbo no PASSADO (came), não na base. Diferente de "didn't / did not + base".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I never visited Japan, but I went to China. My sister didn't travel last year. She stayed home."
    **Question**: Onde a irmã ficou?
    **Answer**: home
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "She stayed home".

## Unit N05 — Tudo no passado

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Integration past simple (positive/negative/question/WH) | **Tense**: PAST · simple
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você narra uma história completa com afirmativas, negativas e perguntas no passado.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ to the party last night."
   **Options**: went / goed / didn't went
   **Answer**: went
   **Explanation**: Past simple afirmativo irregular: "went".

2. **multiple_choice** — "She _____ enjoy the music."
   **Options**: didn't / wasn't / doesn't
   **Answer**: didn't
   **Explanation**: Passado negativo: didn't + base.

3. **word_bank** — "_____ you have fun?"
   **Choices**: Did / Do / Were / Have
   **Answer**: Did
   **Explanation**: Past question com Did.

4. **word_bank** — "Where _____ they go?"
   **Choices**: did / do / were / are
   **Answer**: did
   **Explanation**: WH no passado + Did.

5. **fill_gap** — "I _____ a great time."
   **Hint**: Passado de "have"
   **Answer**: had
   **Explanation**: "I had a great time" — irregular afirmativo.

6. **fill_gap** — "She _____ stay long."
   **Hint**: Past negativo padrão
   **Answer**: didn't
   **Explanation**: "She didn't / did not stay" — negativa.

7. **fill_gap** — "Why _____ he leave?"
   **Hint**: WH past
   **Answer**: did
   **Explanation**: "Why did he leave?" — pergunta WH no passado.

8. **fix_error** — "She didn't went home."
   **Hint**: Após didn't, base
   **Answer**: She didn't go home.
   **Explanation**: didn't + base, não "didn't / did not went".

9. **fix_error** — "Did you saw the movie?"
   **Hint**: Após Did, base
   **Answer**: Did you see the movie?
   **Explanation**: Did + sujeito + base. "See", não "saw".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I went to a concert. I didn't stay long. Did my friend come? No, she didn't. What did she do? She worked late."
    **Question**: O amigo veio ao show?
    **Answer**: no
    **Explanation**: The text says "No, she didn't / did not".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I went to the party."** — affirmative
2. **"She didn't come."** — negative
3. **"Did you have fun?"** — yes/no question
4. **"Where did they go?"** — WH question
5. **"I had a great time."** — affirmative + adj

## Cross-unit consolidation

Ao terminar M17, o aluno deve usar naturalmente:
- didn't + verbo base (sem -ed, sem irregular)
- Did + sujeito + verbo base?
- WH + did + sujeito + base?
- Never + past verb (sem auxiliar)
- Short answers: Yes, I did / No, I didn't

Esses chunks fecham o passado simples completo. M18 abre o futuro com going to.
