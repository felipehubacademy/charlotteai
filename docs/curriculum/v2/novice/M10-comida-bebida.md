> **Level**: Novice (A1)
> **Block**: A1 Block
> **Units**: 5 (N01–N05)

- A glass of / a cup of / a bottle of / a slice of

## Unit N01 — Contáveis e incontáveis

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: Countable vs uncountable nouns | **Tense**: EXPRESSÕES
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você diferencia comidas que se contam (uma maçã, dois pães) de coisas que não se contam (água, leite).

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I have _____ apples in my bag."
   **Options**: two / much / some apple
   **Answer**: two
   **Explanation**: "Apple" é contável: "one apple, two apples, three apples". Plural com -s.

2. **multiple_choice** — "I drink a lot of _____."
   **Options**: water / waters / a water
   **Answer**: water
   **Explanation**: "Water" é incontável — não tem plural ("waters" é raro). Sem -s. Não usa "a" antes.

3. **word_bank** — "She eats _____ banana every day."
   **Choices**: a / much / two of / many
   **Answer**: a
   **Explanation**: Singular contável: "a banana" (uma banana). Para plurais usaria "two bananas".

4. **word_bank** — "We need _____ milk."
   **Choices**: some / a / many / one
   **Answer**: some
   **Explanation**: "Milk" incontável → "some milk" (um pouco de leite). Não usa "a" nem "many".

5. **fill_gap** — "I'd like _____ bread, please."
   **Hint**: Quantidade indefinida com incontável
   **Answer**: some
   **Explanation**: "Bread" é incontável. "Some bread" = "um pouco de pão / pão" (quantidade não especificada).

6. **fill_gap** — "She has three _____ in her lunch."
   **Hint**: Plural de "sandwich"
   **Answer**: sandwiches
   **Explanation**: "Sandwich" termina em -ch, plural com -es: "sandwiches". Contável.

7. **fill_gap** — "Can I have _____ juice?"
   **Hint**: Quantidade indefinida (juice = incontável)
   **Answer**: some
   **Explanation**: "Juice" é incontável → "some juice". Padrão pra pedir bebida.

8. **fix_error** — "I'd like a water, please."
   **Hint**: "Water" é incontável
   **Answer**: I'd like some water, please.
   **Explanation**: "Water" é incontável, não usa "a". Use "some water" ou "a glass of water" (com unidade).

9. **fix_error** — "I have two breads."
   **Hint**: "Bread" é incontável
   **Answer**: I have some bread.
   **Explanation**: "Bread" não tem plural com -s. Pra quantificar: "two pieces of bread", "some bread", "a slice of bread".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "For breakfast I have two eggs, some bread, and a cup of coffee. My sister has one apple and some yogurt."
    **Question**: Quantos ovos o narrador tem?
    **Answer**: two
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "I have two eggs".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I have two apples."** — countable plural
2. **"I drink a lot of water."** — uncountable
3. **"Can I have some bread?"** — uncountable + some
4. **"I'd like a sandwich."** — countable singular + a
5. **"She eats some cheese every day."** — uncountable habit

## Unit N02 — Some ou any?

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: some (afirmativas/pedidos) vs any (negativas/perguntas) | **Tense**: EXPRESSÕES
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você abre a geladeira e descreve o que tem (some) ou não tem (any).

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I have _____ apples in the fridge."
   **Options**: some / any / much
   **Answer**: some
   **Explanation**: Afirmativa → "some". "I have some apples" = "Tenho algumas maçãs". Some pra positivo.

2. **multiple_choice** — "We don't have _____ milk."
   **Options**: any / some / much
   **Answer**: any
   **Explanation**: Negativa → "any". "We don't / do not have any milk" = "Não temos leite (nenhum)". Any pra negativo.

3. **word_bank** — "Is there _____ juice?"
   **Choices**: any / some / many / one
   **Answer**: any
   **Explanation**: Pergunta → "any". "Is there any juice?" = "Tem suco?". Any em perguntas.

4. **word_bank** — "Can I have _____ water, please?"
   **Choices**: some / any / many / much
   **Answer**: some
   **Explanation**: Pedido educado → "some" (exceção à regra de pergunta). "Can I have some water?" — chunk fixo pra pedidos.

5. **fill_gap** — "There are _____ cookies on the table."
   **Hint**: Afirmativa (positiva)
   **Answer**: some
   **Explanation**: "There are some cookies" — afirmativa usa some.

6. **fill_gap** — "I don't have _____ time today."
   **Hint**: Negativa
   **Answer**: any
   **Explanation**: "I don't / do not have any time" — negativa usa any (= nenhum/nenhuma).

7. **fill_gap** — "Do you have _____ questions?"
   **Hint**: Pergunta
   **Answer**: any
   **Explanation**: "Do you have any questions?" — pergunta usa any. Chunk muito comum.

8. **fix_error** — "I have any bananas in my bag."
   **Hint**: Afirmativa pede "some"
   **Answer**: I have some bananas in my bag.
   **Explanation**: Afirmativa usa SOME, não any. "I have some bananas".

9. **fix_error** — "We don't have some bread."
   **Hint**: Negativa pede "any"
   **Answer**: We don't have any bread.
   **Explanation**: Negativa usa ANY, não some. "We don't / do not have any bread".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I have some bread but I don't have any milk. Is there any cheese? Yes, there's some cheese in the fridge."
    **Question**: O que o narrador NÃO tem?
    **Answer**: milk
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "I don't / do not have any milk".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I have some apples."** — affirmative some
2. **"We don't have any milk."** — negative any
3. **"Is there any juice?"** — question any
4. **"Can I have some water?"** — polite request some
5. **"There's some bread on the table."** — affirmative + there's

## Unit N03 — Quanto custa, quantos tem

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: How much (uncountable, price) vs How many (countable) | **Tense**: EXPRESSÕES
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você pergunta quantidade ou preço em uma loja ou no mercado.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "_____ apples do you have?"
   **Options**: How many / How much / How
   **Answer**: How many
   **Explanation**: "Apples" é contável → "How many". "How much" é pra incontável ou preço.

2. **multiple_choice** — "_____ water do you drink?"
   **Options**: How much / How many / How
   **Answer**: How much
   **Explanation**: "Water" é incontável → "How much". Quantidade de líquido/massa: how much.

3. **word_bank** — "_____ is this sandwich?"
   **Choices**: How much / How many / How / What
   **Answer**: How much
   **Explanation**: Preço sempre "How much": "How much is it?" = "Quanto custa?". Mesmo que o item seja contável, o preço é uncountable.

4. **word_bank** — "_____ eggs are in the fridge?"
   **Choices**: How many / How much / How / What
   **Answer**: How many
   **Explanation**: "Eggs" contável → "How many".

5. **fill_gap** — "_____ milk do we need?"
   **Hint**: Quantidade de incontável
   **Answer**: How much
   **Explanation**: "Milk" incontável → "How much". "How much milk?".

6. **fill_gap** — "_____ cookies are there?"
   **Hint**: Quantidade de contável
   **Answer**: How many
   **Explanation**: "Cookies" contável → "How many cookies?".

7. **fill_gap** — "_____ does this coffee cost?"
   **Hint**: Preço
   **Answer**: How much
   **Explanation**: Preço sempre "how much". "How much does it cost?" — pergunta padrão.

8. **fix_error** — "How many milk do you drink?"
   **Hint**: Milk é incontável
   **Answer**: How much milk do you drink?
   **Explanation**: Milk não conta — "how much milk". "How many" só pra plurais contáveis.

9. **fix_error** — "How much sandwiches are there?"
   **Hint**: Sandwiches é contável (plural)
   **Answer**: How many sandwiches are there?
   **Explanation**: Plurais contáveis usam "how many". "How many sandwiches?".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "Q: How much is the coffee? A: It's $3. Q: How many cookies do you want? A: Three, please."
    **Question**: Quantos biscoitos o cliente quer?
    **Answer**: three
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "Three, please".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"How many apples?"** — countable question
2. **"How much milk?"** — uncountable question
3. **"How much is it?"** — price chunk
4. **"How many people are here?"** — countable plural
5. **"How much sugar do you want?"** — uncountable + want

## Unit N04 — No restaurante

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: Restaurant chunks (I'd like, Can I have, the bill) | **Tense**: EXPRESSÕES
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você está num restaurante fazendo pedidos.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "_____ a coffee, please."
   **Options**: I'd like / I want / Give me
   **Answer**: I'd like
   **Explanation**: "I'd / I would like" (I would like) é o chunk educado pra pedir. "I want" é direto demais; "Give me" é rude.

2. **multiple_choice** — "_____ I have the menu?"
   **Options**: Can / Do / Are
   **Answer**: Can
   **Explanation**: "Can I have...?" é pedido educado padrão em restaurantes. Sem "do".

3. **word_bank** — "I'd like _____ pizza, please."
   **Choices**: a / the / some / one
   **Answer**: a
   **Explanation**: "A pizza" — pedido singular contável. "I'd / I would like a pizza".

4. **word_bank** — "_____ the bill, please."
   **Choices**: Can I have / I want / Bring me / Give me
   **Answer**: Can I have
   **Explanation**: "Can I have the bill, please?" — pedido educado padrão pra fechar conta.

5. **fill_gap** — "I'd _____ a sandwich and a juice."
   **Hint**: Forma curta de "would"
   **Answer**: like

## Unit N05 — Tudo sobre comida

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: Integration of countable/uncountable + some/any + how much/many + restaurant chunks | **Tense**: EXPRESSÕES
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você integra tudo em conversas sobre comida — preferências, pedidos, refeições, descrições.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I'd like _____ tea, please."
   **Options**: some / a / many / one
   **Answer**: some
   **Explanation**: "Tea" incontável → "some tea". Pedido educado com I'd like.

2. **multiple_choice** — "We don't have _____ eggs."
   **Options**: any / some / much / one
   **Answer**: any
   **Explanation**: Negativa + contável plural → "any eggs".

3. **word_bank** — "_____ water do you drink every day?"
   **Choices**: How much / How many / How / What
   **Answer**: How much
   **Explanation**: Quantidade de incontável → "How much".

4. **word_bank** — "Can I have _____ apple, please?"
   **Choices**: an / some / many / one
   **Answer**: an
   **Explanation**: "An apple" — singular contável (apple começa com vogal → "an").

5. **fill_gap** — "Is there _____ milk in the fridge?"
   **Hint**: Pergunta com incontável
   **Answer**: any
   **Explanation**: Pergunta + incontável → "any". "Is there any milk?".

6. **fill_gap** — "I have _____ cookies and _____ juice."
   **Hint**: Afirmativa (positiva) com ambos
   **Answer**: some / some
   **Explanation**: Afirmativa usa "some" tanto pra contável quanto incontável. "I have some cookies and some juice".

7. **fill_gap** — "_____ is this sandwich?"
   **Hint**: Preço
   **Answer**: How much
   **Explanation**: Preço sempre "how much". "How much is this sandwich?".

8. **fix_error** — "I'd like one tea."
   **Hint**: Tea é incontável
   **Answer**: I'd like some tea.
   **Explanation**: Tea = incontável. Não usa "one". Use "some tea" ou "a cup of tea".

9. **fix_error** — "How many milk do we have?"
   **Hint**: Milk é incontável
   **Answer**: How much milk do we have?
   **Explanation**: Milk incontável → "How much".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I'd like a sandwich and some water. We don't have any cookies. How many apples are there? Three."
    **Question**: Quantas maçãs tem?
    **Answer**: three
    **Explanation**: O texto responde "Three" à pergunta.

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I'd like some coffee, please."** — polite + uncountable
2. **"Do you have any cookies?"** — question + countable plural
3. **"How much is the sandwich?"** — price chunk

## Cross-unit consolidation

Ao terminar M10, o aluno deve usar naturalmente:
- Distinção contável (a, an, número, plural) vs incontável (some, much)
- Some em afirmativas e pedidos / Any em negativas e perguntas
- How many em contável plural / How much em incontável + preço
- "I'd like" e "Can I have" como pedidos educados
- "Can I have the bill" pra fechar conta

Esses chunks abrem M11 (Compras), onde o aluno usa I'd like, this/that, e prepositions of place em lojas.
