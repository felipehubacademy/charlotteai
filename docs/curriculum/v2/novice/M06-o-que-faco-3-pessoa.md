# Module M06 — O que faço (3ª pessoa)

> **Level**: Novice (A1)
> **Block**: A1 Block
> **Units**: 5 (N01–N05)
> **Theme**: Falar sobre o que outras pessoas fazem — 3ª pessoa do present simple
> **Module goal**: Aluno sai sabendo conjugar 3ª pessoa singular com -s/-es/-ies, fazer negativas com doesn't, perguntar com Does/Do, e usar tudo isso pra falar de família, amigos e colegas. Este é o módulo mais denso pra lusófonos.
> **Connects to**: M07 (Habilidades) — depois de dominar todo o present simple, aprende a falar do que sabe fazer (can/can't).

## Module chunks introduced (~40)

- works / studies / lives / plays / goes / watches / does / has / drinks
- spelling rules: -s (works), -es (watches/goes/does), -ies (studies/cries)
- I don't / you don't / we don't / they don't
- He doesn't / she doesn't / it doesn't
- Do you...? / Do they...? / Do we...?
- Does he...? / Does she...? / Does it...?
- What do you do? / What does she do?
- Where do you work? / Where does he live?
- When do they study?
- How does she go to work?
- Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
- Yes, she does. / No, he doesn't.
- He works in + place
- She studies + subject
- He lives in + place
- She plays + sport/instrument

## Unit N01 — Ela trabalha, ele estuda

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: Present simple 3ª pessoa singular (-s, -es, -ies)
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você fala sobre o que sua irmã, irmão ou amigo faz no dia-a-dia.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "She _____ in a school."
   **Options**: works / work / working
   **Answer**: works
   **Explanation**: Na 3ª pessoa singular (he/she/it) o verbo leva -s no present simple: "She works". Com I/you/we/they é forma base ("I work"). Esse é o erro mais comum pra lusófonos.

2. **multiple_choice** — "He _____ English every day."
   **Options**: studies / study / studyes
   **Answer**: studies
   **Explanation**: Verbos terminados em consoante + y trocam y por -ies: study → studies, cry → cries. "He studies", não "studys" nem "studyes".

3. **word_bank** — "Ana _____ in a hospital."
   **Choices**: works / work / working / worked
   **Answer**: works
   **Explanation**: Ana = "she" (3ª pessoa singular). Verbo + -s: "Ana works".

4. **word_bank** — "He _____ TV every night."
   **Choices**: watches / watch / watchs / watched
   **Answer**: watches
   **Explanation**: Verbos terminados em -ch, -sh, -ss, -x, -o levam -es: "watches", "goes", "fixes". Não é "watchs".

5. **fill_gap** — "She _____ coffee in the morning."
   **Hint**: Verbo "beber" na 3ª pessoa singular
   **Answer**: drinks
   **Explanation**: "She drinks" — 3ª pessoa leva -s. Drink + s = drinks.

6. **fill_gap** — "Lucas _____ to school by bus."
   **Hint**: Verbo "ir" na 3ª pessoa singular
   **Answer**: goes
   **Explanation**: "Go" termina em -o, leva -es: "goes". Lucas = he, então "Lucas goes".

7. **fill_gap** — "My mother _____ in a small office."
   **Hint**: Verbo "trabalhar" na 3ª pessoa
   **Answer**: works
   **Explanation**: "My mother" = she — 3ª pessoa singular. "Works" (work + s).

8. **fix_error** — "He work in a bank."
   **Hint**: 3ª pessoa singular leva -s
   **Answer**: He works in a bank.
   **Explanation**: Com "he", "she", "it" o verbo SEMPRE leva -s no present simple. "He works", não "He work". Este é o erro mais clássico de lusófonos — o português não tem -s na 3ª pessoa.

9. **fix_error** — "She studys English."
   **Hint**: -y precedido de consoante vira -ies
   **Answer**: She studies English.
   **Explanation**: Verbos terminados em consoante + y trocam y por -ies. Study → studies, cry → cries, try → tries. Não é "studys".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "Tom is my friend. He works in a bank. He studies German at night. He goes home at six."
    **Question**: Onde Tom trabalha?
    **Answer**: in a bank
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "He works in a bank".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"She works in a hospital."** — 3ª pessoa + -s
2. **"He studies English every day."** — -ies (consoante + y)
3. **"My brother goes to school."** — -es (verbos em -o)
4. **"She watches TV at night."** — -es (verbos em -ch)
5. **"Lucas drinks coffee in the morning."** — -s regular

## Unit N02 — Eu não faço, ela não faz

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: Negative present simple (don't / doesn't)
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você conta o que você (e outros) NÃO fazem — hábitos negativos, restrições.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ work on Sundays."
   **Options**: don't / doesn't / am not
   **Answer**: don't
   **Explanation**: Com I/you/we/they usa "don't / do not" + verbo base: "I don't / do not work". "Doesn't / Does not" é só pra he/she/it.

2. **multiple_choice** — "She _____ eat meat."
   **Options**: doesn't / don't / not
   **Answer**: doesn't
   **Explanation**: Com he/she/it usa "doesn't / does not" + verbo base: "She doesn't / does not eat". Atenção: depois de "doesn't / does not" o verbo NÃO leva -s.

3. **word_bank** — "We _____ live in Brazil."
   **Choices**: don't / doesn't / not / no
   **Answer**: don't
   **Explanation**: "We" é plural → "don't / do not". "We don't / do not live" — verbo na forma base após don't.

4. **word_bank** — "He _____ drink coffee."
   **Choices**: doesn't / don't / no / not
   **Answer**: doesn't
   **Explanation**: "He" → "doesn't / does not". Após "doesn't / does not", verbo base: "doesn't / does not drink", não "doesn't / does not drinks".

5. **fill_gap** — "I _____ study French."
   **Hint**: Negativo de "I"
   **Answer**: don't
   **Explanation**: "I don't / do not study" — eu não estudo. "Don't / Do not" + forma base do verbo.

6. **fill_gap** — "My brother _____ work on weekends."
   **Hint**: Negativo para 3ª pessoa singular
   **Answer**: doesn't
   **Explanation**: "My brother" = he → "doesn't / does not work". Note: verbo base após "doesn't / does not" (não "doesn't / does not works").

7. **fill_gap** — "They _____ watch TV at night."
   **Hint**: Negativo para plural
   **Answer**: don't
   **Explanation**: "They" plural → "don't / do not watch". Sempre forma base após "don't / do not".

8. **fix_error** — "She don't like coffee."
   **Hint**: She é 3ª pessoa singular
   **Answer**: She doesn't like coffee.
   **Explanation**: She → "doesn't / does not" (não "don't / do not"). Apenas I/you/we/they usam "don't / do not". He/she/it sempre "doesn't / does not".

9. **fix_error** — "He doesn't works late."
   **Hint**: Após "doesn't" o verbo fica base
   **Answer**: He doesn't work late.
   **Explanation**: "Doesn't / Does not" já carrega o -s da 3ª pessoa. O verbo principal volta pra forma base: "doesn't / does not work" (não "doesn't / does not works"). Erro super comum.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I don't drink coffee. My brother doesn't eat meat. We don't work on Sundays. Ana doesn't study at night."
    **Question**: O que Ana não faz?
    **Answer**: study at night
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "Ana doesn't / does not study at night".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I don't drink coffee."** — own habit negative
2. **"She doesn't work on Mondays."** — 3rd person negative
3. **"He doesn't like pizza."** — preference negative
4. **"We don't live in São Paulo."** — plural negative
5. **"My sister doesn't study French."** — family member negative

## Unit N04 — Onde, quando, como

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: WH-questions with do/does (Where do you...? What does she...?)
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Entrevista informal — você faz perguntas WH pra alguém sobre vida e rotina.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "Where _____ you work?"
   **Options**: do / does / are
   **Answer**: do
   **Explanation**: WH-question: "WH + auxiliar + sujeito + verbo base". "Where do you work?" — "you" usa "do".

2. **multiple_choice** — "What _____ she do for a living?"
   **Options**: does / do / is
   **Answer**: does
   **Explanation**: "What does she do?" — "she" 3ª pessoa, usa "does". Verbo base "do" no fim (não "does").

3. **word_bank** — "When _____ they have dinner?"
   **Choices**: do / does / are / have
   **Answer**: do
   **Explanation**: "When do they have dinner?" — "they" plural usa "do".

4. **word_bank** — "Where _____ Lucas live?"
   **Choices**: does / do / is / has
   **Answer**: does
   **Explanation**: Lucas = he → "Does". "Where does Lucas live?". Verbo base após "does".

5. **fill_gap** — "Why _____ you study English?"
   **Hint**: Auxiliar para "you"
   **Answer**: do
   **Explanation**: "Why do you study?" — pergunta WH com "you" usa "do".

6. **fill_gap** — "How _____ your sister go to school?"
   **Hint**: Auxiliar para 3ª pessoa singular
   **Answer**: does
   **Explanation**: "Your sister" = she → "How does your sister go?".

7. **fill_gap** — "What time _____ he wake up?"
   **Hint**: Auxiliar para he
   **Answer**: does
   **Explanation**: "He" 3ª pessoa → "does". "What time does he wake up?".

8. **fix_error** — "Where does you work?"
   **Hint**: Auxiliar errado para you
   **Answer**: Where do you work?
   **Explanation**: "You" usa "do", não "does". Apenas he/she/it usa "does".

9. **fix_error** — "What she does for a living?"
   **Hint**: Ordem da pergunta WH
   **Answer**: What does she do for a living?
   **Explanation**: WH-question precisa do auxiliar ANTES do sujeito: "What does she do?" — não "What she does?". Note também o verbo base "do" no final.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "Q: Where do you work? A: I work in a hospital. Q: What time do you start? A: I start at eight. Q: When does your sister study? A: She studies at night."
    **Question**: Quando a irmã estuda?
    **Answer**: at night
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "She studies at night".

## Cross-unit consolidation

Ao terminar M06, o aluno deve usar naturalmente:
- 3ª pessoa singular com -s/-es/-ies: works, studies, watches, goes, does
- Negativa: I/you/we/they don't + base | He/she/it doesn't + base (NUNCA "doesn't works")
- Pergunta Yes/No: Do + sujeito + base | Does + sujeito + base
- Pergunta WH: WH + do/does + sujeito + base (Where do you work? / What does she do?)
- Short answers: Yes, I do / No, she doesn't

Esses chunks fecham o ciclo completo do present simple. M07 abre um capítulo novo — habilidades com can/can't.
