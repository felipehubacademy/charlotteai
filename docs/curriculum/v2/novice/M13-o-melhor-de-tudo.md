# Module M13 — O melhor de tudo

> **Level**: Novice (A2)
> **Block**: A2 Block
> **Units**: 5 (N01–N05)
> **Theme**: Superlativos e ordinais — destacar o melhor, o pior, o primeiro
> **Module goal**: Aluno sai sabendo usar superlativos (-est, the most), irregulares (best/worst) e ordinais (first/second/third) pra rankings e destaques.
> **Connects to**: M14 (Onde estive ontem) — depois de destacar o melhor, fala do passado com was/were.

## Module chunks introduced (~30)

- the + adj-est (the tallest, the biggest, the easiest)
- the most + long adj (the most expensive, the most interesting)
- the best / the worst (irregular)
- the most/the least + adj
- first, second, third, fourth, fifth, tenth, twentieth, last
- in the world / in my class / in my family
- ever (best ever, worst ever)
- of all / by far the + superlative

## Unit N01 — O mais alto, o maior

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Superlatives with short adjectives (the -est) | **Tense**: SUPERLATIVO
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você destaca o mais alto, o maior, o mais barato da família, da turma, do bairro.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "He is _____ in our class."
   **Options**: the tallest / tallest / more tall
   **Answer**: the tallest
   **Explanation**: Superlativo curto: "the + adj-est". "The tallest" — destaca o máximo entre um grupo.

2. **multiple_choice** — "This is _____ phone in the store."
   **Options**: the cheapest / cheapest / the more cheap
   **Answer**: the cheapest
   **Explanation**: "Cheap" → "the cheapest". Sempre "the" antes do superlativo.

3. **word_bank** — "She is _____ in her family."
   **Choices**: the youngest / youngest / more young / younger
   **Answer**: the youngest
   **Explanation**: "Young" → "youngest" (regular). "The youngest" = a mais nova.

4. **word_bank** — "Today is _____ day of the year."
   **Choices**: the hottest / hottest / more hot / hotter
   **Answer**: the hottest
   **Explanation**: "Hot" → "hottest" (dobra o t). "The hottest day".

5. **fill_gap** — "She is _____ in the team — nobody beats her time."
   **Hint**: Superlativo de "fast"
   **Answer**: the fastest
   **Explanation**: "Fast" → "fastest". "The fastest" = a mais rápida.

6. **fill_gap** — "This is _____ exam of the semester — only basic questions."
   **Hint**: Superlativo de "easy"
   **Answer**: the easiest
   **Explanation**: "Easy" → "easiest" (y → iest). "The easiest exam".

7. **fill_gap** — "São Paulo is _____ city in Brazil."
   **Hint**: Superlativo de "big"
   **Answer**: the biggest
   **Explanation**: "Big" → "biggest" (dobra o g). "The biggest city in Brazil".

8. **fix_error** — "He is tallest in our class."
   **Hint**: Falta artigo
   **Answer**: He is the tallest in our class.
   **Explanation**: Superlativo SEMPRE com "the": "THE tallest". Sem "the" é erro comum.

9. **fix_error** — "She is the most short."
   **Hint**: Short é curto, usa -est
   **Answer**: She is the shortest.
   **Explanation**: "Short" (1 sílaba) → "the shortest" (com -est). Não "the most short" (que seria pra long adj).

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "My brother is the tallest in our family. My sister is the youngest. I'm the oldest. We all live in the biggest house on the street."
    **Question**: Quem é o mais alto?
    **Answer**: my brother
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "My brother is the tallest".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"He is the tallest."** — tallest
2. **"She is the youngest in the family."** — youngest
3. **"Today is the hottest day."** — hottest
4. **"This is the cheapest option."** — cheapest
5. **"São Paulo is the biggest city in Brazil."** — biggest

## Unit N02 — O mais interessante

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Superlatives with long adjectives (the most + adj) | **Tense**: SUPERLATIVO
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você destaca o mais interessante, mais caro, mais bonito de uma lista ou categoria.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "This is _____ movie of the year."
   **Options**: the most interesting / the interestingest / most interesting
   **Answer**: the most interesting
   **Explanation**: Adjetivo longo → "the most + adj". "The most interesting", não "interestingest".

2. **multiple_choice** — "She is _____ person in the room."
   **Options**: the most beautiful / the beautifulest / most beautiful
   **Answer**: the most beautiful
   **Explanation**: "Beautiful" → "the most beautiful". Long adj sempre com "most".

3. **word_bank** — "Paris is _____ city."
   **Choices**: the most expensive / most expensive / the expensivest / expensiver
   **Answer**: the most expensive
   **Explanation**: "Expensive" → "the most expensive". Sempre com "the" e "most".

4. **word_bank** — "This is _____ problem of all."
   **Choices**: the most difficult / the difficultest / most difficult / difficulter
   **Answer**: the most difficult
   **Explanation**: "Difficult" (3 sílabas) → "the most difficult".

5. **fill_gap** — "She is _____ teacher in the school — she never gets angry."
   **Hint**: Superlativo longo de "patient"
   **Answer**: the most patient
   **Explanation**: "Patient" → "the most patient" (2 sílabas+, usa "most").

6. **fill_gap** — "It's _____ topic of the year — everyone is talking about it."
   **Hint**: Superlativo longo de "popular"
   **Answer**: the most popular
   **Explanation**: "Popular" → "the most popular". Sempre "most" antes de adj longo.

7. **fill_gap** — "This is _____ book in the library — I almost fell asleep."
   **Hint**: Superlativo longo de "boring"
   **Answer**: the most boring
   **Explanation**: "Boring" (2 sílabas) → "the most boring".

8. **fix_error** — "He is the interestingest person."
   **Hint**: "Interesting" é longo
   **Answer**: He is the most interesting person.
   **Explanation**: "Interestingest" não existe. Sempre "the most interesting" pra adj longo.

9. **fix_error** — "She is most beautiful."
   **Hint**: Falta "the"
   **Answer**: She is the most beautiful.
   **Explanation**: Superlativo sempre com "THE": "the most beautiful". Sem "the" é erro.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "This is the most expensive restaurant in town. The food is the most delicious in the city. The service is also the most professional."
    **Question**: Como é o atendimento?
    **Answer**: the most professional
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "The service is also the most professional".

## Unit N03 — O melhor, o pior

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Irregular superlatives (the best, the worst, the most/least) | **Tense**: SUPERLATIVO
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você destaca o melhor e o pior em listas de favoritos.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "This is _____ coffee in town."
   **Options**: the best / the goodest / the more good
   **Answer**: the best
   **Explanation**: "Good" → "the best" (irregular). Não "goodest" nem "more good".

2. **multiple_choice** — "Today is _____ day of the week."
   **Options**: the worst / the baddest / the more bad
   **Answer**: the worst
   **Explanation**: "Bad" → "the worst" (irregular). Memorizar: bad/worse/worst.

3. **word_bank** — "She is _____ player in the team."
   **Choices**: the best / the goodest / best / most good
   **Answer**: the best
   **Explanation**: "The best" — irregular superlativo de "good". Sempre com "the".

4. **word_bank** — "This is _____ movie of the year."
   **Choices**: the worst / the most bad / the baddest / worst
   **Answer**: the worst
   **Explanation**: "The worst" — irregular superlativo de "bad".

5. **fill_gap** — "He is _____ teacher in the school — students love his classes."
   **Hint**: Irregular superlativo de "good"
   **Answer**: the best
   **Explanation**: "The best" — superlativo de "good". Decora a lista: good/better/best.

6. **fill_gap** — "It's _____ thing about Mondays — waking up so early!"
   **Hint**: Irregular superlativo de "bad"
   **Answer**: the worst
   **Explanation**: "The worst" — superlativo de "bad". Lista: bad/worse/worst.

7. **fill_gap** — "She has _____ ideas of all."
   **Hint**: Quantidade máxima (mais)
   **Answer**: the most
   **Explanation**: "The most ideas" = mais ideias. "The most + noun" pra quantidades.

8. **fix_error** — "This is the goodest restaurant."
   **Hint**: Good é irregular
   **Answer**: This is the best restaurant.
   **Explanation**: "Goodest" não existe. Irregular: "the best".

9. **fix_error** — "Today is the more bad day."
   **Hint**: Bad é irregular
   **Answer**: Today is the worst day.
   **Explanation**: "Bad" → "the worst" (irregular). Não "more bad" nem "baddest".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "Ana is the best singer in our class. Lucas is the worst at math but the best at art. We all agree — she has the most talent."
    **Question**: Quem é o melhor em arte?
    **Answer**: Lucas
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "Lucas is... the best at art".

## Unit N04 — Primeiro, segundo, terceiro

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Ordinals (first, second, third...) | **Tense**: SUPERLATIVO
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você ordena coisas em sequência — andares, posições, datas, listas.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I live on the _____ floor."
   **Options**: third / three / threeth
   **Answer**: third
   **Explanation**: Ordinais têm formas especiais: 3 = "three", 3rd = "third" (não "threeth"). Decorar: 1st first, 2nd second, 3rd third.

2. **multiple_choice** — "She came in _____ place."
   **Options**: first / one / oneth
   **Answer**: first
   **Explanation**: 1 = "one", 1st = "first". Forma especial. "First place" = primeiro lugar.

3. **word_bank** — "It's my _____ time visiting Brazil."
   **Choices**: second / two / twoth / twoeth
   **Answer**: second
   **Explanation**: 2nd = "second" (forma única). Não "twoth".

4. **word_bank** — "The party is on the _____ of December."
   **Choices**: twenty-fifth / twenty-five / twentyfive / twenty-fifth-th
   **Answer**: twenty-fifth
   **Explanation**: 25th = "twenty-fifth". Compostos seguem padrão: 5 + th = fifth (não fifteenth). Datas usam ordinais.

5. **fill_gap** — "I live on the _____ floor."
   **Hint**: Ordinal de 5
   **Answer**: fifth
   **Explanation**: 5th = "fifth" (forma irregular). Note: "fifteenth" é 15th.

6. **fill_gap** — "January is the _____ month of the year."
   **Hint**: Ordinal de 1

## Cross-unit consolidation

Ao terminar M13, o aluno deve usar naturalmente:
- Superlativos curtos: the tallest, the biggest, the easiest
- Superlativos longos: the most expensive, the most interesting
- Irregulares: the best, the worst, the most/least
- Ordinais: first, second, third, fifth, twentieth
- Sempre "the" antes do superlativo
- Sem misturar "the most tall" ou "the goodest"

Esses chunks fecham comparações. M14 abre o passado com was/were.
