# Module M12 — Comparações

> **Level**: Novice (A2)
> **Block**: A2 Block
> **Units**: 5 (N01–N05)
> **Theme**: Comparar pessoas, coisas e lugares
> **Module goal**: Aluno sai sabendo formar comparativos com -er (curtos) e more + adjetivo (longos), usar irregulares (better/worse), e comparar pessoas, lugares e produtos naturalmente.
> **Connects to**: M13 (O melhor de tudo) — depois de comparar dois, aprende a destacar O melhor/pior (superlatives).

## Module chunks introduced (~35)

- comparativos curtos: -er + than (taller, smaller, faster, cheaper, older, younger)
- regras spelling: big → bigger, hot → hotter, easy → easier, nice → nicer
- comparativos longos: more + adjective + than (more expensive, more beautiful, more interesting)
- irregulares: good → better, bad → worse, far → farther
- much + comparative (much bigger, much better)
- a little / a bit + comparative
- the same as / as + adj + as (equality intro)
- big / small, fast / slow, cheap / expensive, nice / boring, hot / cold, easy / hard

## Unit N01 — Mais alto, mais baixo

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Short adjectives + -er than | **Tense**: COMPARATIVO
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você compara pessoas (alturas), coisas (preços) e lugares (tamanhos) usando adjetivos curtos.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "He is _____ than me."
   **Options**: taller / more tall / talls
   **Answer**: taller
   **Explanation**: Adjetivos curtos (1 sílaba) usam -er: tall → taller. "More tall" só pra adjetivos longos.

2. **multiple_choice** — "This shirt is _____ than that one."
   **Options**: cheaper / more cheap / cheap
   **Answer**: cheaper
   **Explanation**: "Cheap" (1 sílaba) → "cheaper". Sempre -er pra curtos.

3. **word_bank** — "I'm _____ than my brother."
   **Choices**: older / more old / oldest / old
   **Answer**: older
   **Explanation**: "Old" → "older". "More old" não existe; "oldest" é superlativo.

4. **word_bank** — "São Paulo is _____ than Rio."
   **Choices**: bigger / more big / biggest / big
   **Answer**: bigger
   **Explanation**: "Big" → "bigger" (dobra o g antes de -er). Regra de spelling: consoante-vogal-consoante dobra.

5. **fill_gap** — "She is _____ than her sister."
   **Hint**: Comparativo de "young"
   **Answer**: younger
   **Explanation**: "Young" → "younger" (adiciona -er regularmente).

6. **fill_gap** — "Today is _____ than yesterday."
   **Hint**: Comparativo de "hot"
   **Answer**: hotter
   **Explanation**: "Hot" → "hotter" (dobra o t). Mesma regra de "big → bigger".

7. **fill_gap** — "This test is _____ than the last one."
   **Hint**: Comparativo de "easy"
   **Answer**: easier
   **Explanation**: "Easy" → "easier" (y → ier). Adjetivos em -y trocam y por -ier.

8. **fix_error** — "He is taller as me."
   **Hint**: Conector errado
   **Answer**: He is taller than me.
   **Explanation**: Comparativo usa "THAN", não "as". "Taller than" — sempre "than" em comparações com -er.

9. **fix_error** — "She is more tall than him."
   **Hint**: "Tall" é curto, usa -er
   **Answer**: She is taller than him.
   **Explanation**: "Tall" tem 1 sílaba → -er, não "more". "Taller", não "more tall".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I'm taller than my brother but he's older than me. Our sister is younger than both of us. She's also faster — she runs every morning."
    **Question**: Quem é mais velho que o narrador?
    **Answer**: my brother
    **Accepts**: her/his brother
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "he's / he is older than me" se referindo ao irmão.

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"He is taller than me."** — height comparison
2. **"This shirt is cheaper than that one."** — price
3. **"I'm older than my brother."** — age comparison
4. **"São Paulo is bigger than Rio."** — size

## Unit N03 — Melhor, pior, mais, menos

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Irregular comparatives (better/worse/more/less/farther) | **Tense**: COMPARATIVO
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você compara usando os adjetivos irregulares mais comuns.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "This coffee is _____ than that one."
   **Options**: better / gooder / more good
   **Answer**: better
   **Explanation**: "Good" tem comparativo IRREGULAR: "better" (não "gooder" nem "more good"). Decorar como exceção.

2. **multiple_choice** — "Today is _____ than yesterday."
   **Options**: worse / badder / more bad
   **Answer**: worse
   **Explanation**: "Bad" → "worse" (irregular). Não "badder" (apesar de existir gíria) nem "more bad".

3. **word_bank** — "She has _____ time than me."
   **Choices**: more / much / many / most
   **Answer**: more
   **Explanation**: "More + uncountable" (more time, more money). "Much" não usado em afirmativas; "many" é pra contáveis.

4. **word_bank** — "I have _____ books than him."
   **Choices**: more / much / many / better
   **Answer**: more
   **Explanation**: "More + plural countable" (more books). "Many" usado só em pergunta/negativa.

5. **fill_gap** — "My car is _____ than yours."
   **Hint**: Irregular comparativo de "good"
   **Answer**: better
   **Explanation**: "Good" → "better". Memorize: good/better/best.

6. **fill_gap** — "This movie is _____ than I expected."
   **Hint**: Irregular comparativo de "bad"
   **Answer**: worse
   **Explanation**: "Bad" → "worse". Memorize: bad/worse/worst.

7. **fill_gap** — "He has _____ friends than me."
   **Hint**: Comparativo de "few/little" (mais)
   **Answer**: more
   **Explanation**: "More" é o comparativo de quantidade — tanto countable quanto uncountable.

8. **fix_error** — "She is gooder at math."
   **Hint**: "Good" é irregular
   **Answer**: She is better at math.
   **Explanation**: "Gooder" não existe — irregular vira "better". Sempre "better than".

9. **fix_error** — "This is more bad than yesterday."
   **Hint**: "Bad" tem forma irregular
   **Answer**: This is worse than yesterday.
   **Explanation**: "Bad" → "worse" (forma irregular). Não "more bad".

    **Passage**: "My new phone is better than my old one. The camera is much better but the battery is worse. I have more apps than before."

    **Answer**: worse
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "the battery is worse".

## Unit N04 — Bem mais, um pouco mais

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Modifiers (much/a little/a bit + comparative) | **Tense**: COMPARATIVO
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você suaviza ou intensifica comparações — "muito mais", "um pouco mais", "um pouco menos".

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "She is _____ taller than him."
   **Options**: much / very / so
   **Answer**: much
   **Explanation**: "Much + comparative" = MUITO mais. "Much taller" = muito mais alto. "Very" não usa com comparativo.

2. **multiple_choice** — "This phone is _____ more expensive."
   **Options**: a little / a much / very / quite
   **Answer**: a little
   **Explanation**: "A little + comparative" = um pouco mais. "A little more expensive" = um pouco mais caro.

3. **word_bank** — "I'm _____ a bit older than her."
   **Choices**: just / much / very / so
   **Answer**: just
   **Explanation**: "Just a bit older" = só um pouquinho mais velho. Suaviza a diferença.

4. **word_bank** — "Today is _____ hotter than yesterday."
   **Choices**: much / very / so / quite
   **Answer**: much
   **Explanation**: "Much hotter" = bem mais quente. Intensifica.

5. **fill_gap** — "My car is _____ better than my old one."
   **Hint**: Intensifier (muito mais)
   **Answer**: much
   **Explanation**: "Much better" = bem melhor. Padrão pra intensificar comparativos.

6. **fill_gap** — "She is _____ more patient than I am."
   **Hint**: Modificador suave (um pouco)
   **Answer**: a little
   **Explanation**: "A little more patient" = um pouco mais paciente. Suaviza.

7. **fill_gap** — "This room is _____ smaller than that one."
   **Hint**: Modificador suave (um pouco)
   **Answer**: a bit
   **Explanation**: "A bit smaller" = um pouco menor. Sinônimo de "a little".

8. **fix_error** — "She is very taller than me."
   **Hint**: "Very" não vai com comparativo
   **Answer**: She is much taller than me.
   **Explanation**: Com comparativo usa "much", não "very". "Very + adjetivo simples" (very tall); "Much + comparativo" (much taller).

9. **fix_error** — "I'm a little more better today."
   **Hint**: "Better" é comparativo, não usa "more"
   **Answer**: I'm a little better today.
   **Explanation**: "Better" já é comparativo (good → better). NÃO usa "more better". Apenas "a little/much/a bit + better".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "My new apartment is much bigger than the old one. The kitchen is a little smaller, but the bedroom is much more comfortable."
    **Question**: Como é o quarto?
    **Answer**: much more comfortable
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "the bedroom is much more comfortable".

2. **"It's a little more expensive."** — a little + more
3. **"I'm a bit older than her."** — a bit + comparative
4. **"This is much better."** — much + better

## Unit N05 — Comparando tudo

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Integration of all M12 comparatives | **Tense**: COMPARATIVO
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você combina comparativos curtos, longos e irregulares em descrições completas.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "She is _____ than her sister."
   **Options**: taller / more tall / tallest
   **Answer**: taller
   **Explanation**: Adjetivo curto → -er. "Taller", não "more tall".

2. **multiple_choice** — "This restaurant is _____ than the other one."
   **Options**: more expensive / expensiver / more expensiver
   **Answer**: more expensive
   **Explanation**: Adjetivo longo → "more". "More expensive".

3. **word_bank** — "Today is _____ than yesterday."
   **Choices**: better / gooder / more better / more good
   **Answer**: better
   **Explanation**: "Good" → "better" (irregular).

4. **word_bank** — "My car is _____ faster than yours."
   **Choices**: much / very / so / really
   **Answer**: much
   **Explanation**: "Much + comparative". "Very" não vai com comparativo.

5. **fill_gap** — "This phone is _____ (cheap) than that one."
   **Hint**: Comparativo de cheap
   **Answer**: cheaper
   **Explanation**: "Cheap" (1 sílaba) → "cheaper".

6. **fill_gap** — "She is _____ (intelligent) than I expected."
   **Hint**: Comparativo de adjetivo longo
   **Answer**: more intelligent
   **Explanation**: "Intelligent" (4 sílabas) → "more intelligent".

7. **fill_gap** — "Today is _____ (bad) than yesterday."
   **Hint**: Comparativo irregular de bad
   **Answer**: worse
   **Explanation**: "Bad" → "worse" (irregular).

8. **fix_error** — "She is more taller than me."
   **Hint**: "Tall" não usa "more"
   **Answer**: She is taller than me.
   **Explanation**: Adjetivo curto, sem "more". Apenas "taller".

9. **fix_error** — "This is gooder than that."
   **Hint**: Good é irregular
   **Answer**: This is better than that.
   **Explanation**: Good → better, não "gooder". Irregular.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I'm taller than my brother but he's much stronger. My sister is more patient than both of us. She's a better listener too."
    **Question**: Quem é o melhor ouvinte?
    **Answer**: my sister
    **Accepts**: her sister / the sister
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "She's / She is a better listener" — se referindo à irmã (melhor ouvinte que o falante e o irmão).

## Cross-unit consolidation

Ao terminar M12, o aluno deve usar naturalmente:
- Comparativos curtos: tall → taller, big → bigger, easy → easier
- Comparativos longos: interesting → more interesting, beautiful → more beautiful
- Irregulares: good → better, bad → worse, far → farther
- Modificadores: much/a little/a bit + comparative
- Conector: SEMPRE "than" (não "as")
- Sem misturar: "more taller" e "more better" são erros

Esses chunks abrem M13 (O melhor de tudo), onde o aluno aprende a dizer O melhor/pior (superlativos).
