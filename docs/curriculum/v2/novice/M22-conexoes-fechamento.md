# Module M22 — Conexões e fechamento Novice

> **Level**: Novice (A2)
> **Block**: A2 Block (final)
> **Units**: 5 (N01–N05)
> **Theme**: Linking words + adverbs of manner — fechamento Novice
> **Module goal**: Aluno sai sabendo conectar ideias com and/but/because/so e descrever ações com adverbs of manner (-ly), encerrando o Novice.
> **Connects to**: I01 (Inter) — Eventos no passado com past continuous.

## Module chunks introduced (~25)

- and / but / or / so / because
- although / however
- quickly / slowly / carefully / loudly / quietly / happily / sadly
- -ly endings: regular (slowly), y → ily (happy → happily), irregular (good → well, fast → fast)
- She speaks quickly.
- He drives carefully.
- I work hard, but I also rest.

## Unit N01 — E, mas, ou

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Basic linkers (and, but, or)
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você conecta duas ideias em uma frase só.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I like coffee _____ tea."
   **Options**: and / but / or
   **Answer**: and
   **Explanation**: "And" = soma. "I like coffee AND tea" = ambos.

2. **multiple_choice** — "I like tea _____ I don't like coffee."
   **Options**: but / and / or
   **Answer**: but
   **Explanation**: "But" = contraste. Liga ideia oposta.

3. **word_bank** — "Do you want coffee _____ tea?"
   **Choices**: or / and / but / so
   **Answer**: or
   **Explanation**: "Or" = escolha entre opções.

4. **word_bank** — "I'm tired _____ I'll go to bed."
   **Choices**: so / and / but / or
   **Answer**: so
   **Explanation**: "So" = consequência. "Estou cansado, então vou dormir".

5. **fill_gap** — "He plays soccer _____ basketball."
   **Hint**: Soma de atividades
   **Answer**: and
   **Explanation**: "And" — ambos esportes.

6. **fill_gap** — "I want to travel _____ I don't have money."
   **Hint**: Contraste
   **Answer**: but
   **Explanation**: "But" — quero mas não posso.

7. **fill_gap** — "Tea, coffee, _____ juice?"
   **Hint**: Escolha
   **Answer**: or
   **Explanation**: "Or" — escolher entre.

8. **fix_error** — "I'm tired but I'll go to sleep."
   **Hint**: Resultado lógico pede "so"
   **Answer**: I'm tired, so I'll go to sleep.
   **Explanation**: "So" liga causa-consequência. "But" é contraste — não cabe aqui (cansado e dormir são lógicos, não contrastantes).

9. **fix_error** — "I like dogs and cats but birds."
   **Hint**: Estrutura paralela
   **Answer**: I like dogs, cats, and birds. (or use "but" with opposite idea)
   **Explanation**: "But" sem ideia oposta soa estranho. Use "and" pra lista. "But" exigiria contraste.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I like coffee and tea, but I don't like soda. I drink water all day. Do you prefer coffee or tea?"
    **Question**: Does the speaker like soda?
    **Answer**: no (or "doesn't like")
    **Explanation**: The text says "I don't like soda".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I like coffee and tea."** — and
2. **"I like tea but not coffee."** — but
3. **"Coffee or tea?"** — or
4. **"I'm tired, so I'll sleep."** — so
5. **"I want to travel, but I have no money."** — but contrast

## Unit N02 — Porque e então

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Because (cause) + so (result)
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você explica causas e consequências em suas decisões.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I'm tired _____ I worked all day."
   **Options**: because / so / but
   **Answer**: because
   **Explanation**: "Because" introduz a CAUSA. "I'm tired because I worked" — cansado por trabalhar.

2. **multiple_choice** — "I'm tired _____ I'll rest."
   **Options**: so / because / but
   **Answer**: so
   **Explanation**: "So" introduz a CONSEQUÊNCIA. "I'm tired so I'll rest" — cansado, então descanso.

3. **word_bank** — "She studies a lot _____ she wants to pass."
   **Choices**: because / so / but / and
   **Answer**: because
   **Explanation**: "Because she wants" — explicação/motivo.

4. **word_bank** — "He likes coffee _____ he drinks it every day."
   **Choices**: so / because / but / and
   **Answer**: so
   **Explanation**: "Likes coffee SO drinks" — consequência.

5. **fill_gap** — "I went home early _____ I was sick."
   **Hint**: Motivo (causa)
   **Answer**: because
   **Explanation**: "Because I was sick" — explicação.

6. **fill_gap** — "It was raining, _____ we stayed inside."
   **Hint**: Resultado
   **Answer**: so
   **Explanation**: "So we stayed" — consequência da chuva.

7. **fill_gap** — "She is happy _____ she got a job."
   **Hint**: Causa
   **Answer**: because
   **Explanation**: "Because she got a job" — explicação da felicidade.

8. **fix_error** — "I'm tired so I worked all day."
   **Hint**: Causa pede because
   **Answer**: I'm tired because I worked all day.
   **Explanation**: Trabalhar foi a CAUSA do cansaço. Use "because" pra introduzir causa, não "so".

9. **fix_error** — "It's raining because we'll stay home."
   **Hint**: Consequência pede so
   **Answer**: It's raining, so we'll stay home.
   **Explanation**: Ficar em casa é a CONSEQUÊNCIA da chuva. Use "so" pra consequência.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I love English because it opens doors. I study every day, so I'm making progress. I'll keep going because it's fun."
    **Question**: Why does the speaker love English?
    **Answer**: it opens doors (or "opens doors")
    **Explanation**: The text says "I love English because it opens doors".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I'm tired because I worked all day."** — cause
2. **"I'm tired, so I'll rest."** — result
3. **"She studies because she wants to pass."** — cause
4. **"It rained, so we stayed home."** — result
5. **"He's happy because he got a job."** — cause

## Unit N03 — Rapidamente, cuidadosamente

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Adverbs of manner (-ly)
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você descreve COMO ações são feitas — rápido, devagar, cuidadosamente.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "She speaks _____."
   **Options**: quickly / quick / quicker
   **Answer**: quickly
   **Explanation**: Advérbio = adjetivo + -ly. "Quick" → "quickly".

2. **multiple_choice** — "He drives _____."
   **Options**: carefully / careful / careless
   **Answer**: carefully
   **Explanation**: "Careful" → "carefully". Advérbio descreve como dirige.

3. **word_bank** — "She studies _____."
   **Choices**: seriously / serious / very serious / seriouser
   **Answer**: seriously
   **Explanation**: "Serious" → "seriously" — adicionando -ly.

4. **word_bank** — "He sings _____."
   **Choices**: loudly / loud / louder / loud-ly
   **Answer**: loudly
   **Explanation**: "Loud" → "loudly". Junto, sem hífen.

5. **fill_gap** — "She always laughs _____."
   **Hint**: Advérbio de "happy" (y → ily)
   **Answer**: happily
   **Explanation**: "Happy" → "happily" (y → ily). Regra de spelling.

6. **fill_gap** — "He walks _____ in the morning."
   **Hint**: Advérbio de "slow"
   **Answer**: slowly
   **Explanation**: "Slow" + ly = "slowly".

7. **fill_gap** — "Please speak _____."
   **Hint**: Advérbio de "quiet"
   **Answer**: quietly
   **Explanation**: "Quiet" → "quietly". Manter o "t".

8. **fix_error** — "She speaks quick."
   **Hint**: Falta -ly
   **Answer**: She speaks quickly.
   **Explanation**: "Quick" é adjetivo. Pra modificar o verbo, usa advérbio: "quickly".

9. **fix_error** — "He sings good."
   **Hint**: Good tem advérbio irregular
   **Answer**: He sings well.
   **Explanation**: "Good" (adj) → "well" (advérbio irregular). Não "goodly". "He sings well".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "She speaks slowly and clearly. He drives carefully. They study seriously. We all sing happily."
    **Question**: How does she speak?
    **Answer**: slowly and clearly
    **Explanation**: The text says "She speaks slowly and clearly".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"She speaks quickly."** — adj + ly
2. **"He drives carefully."** — careful + ly
3. **"They study seriously."** — serious + ly
4. **"We sing happily."** — happy → happily
5. **"He sings well."** — irregular (good → well)

## Unit N04 — Embora, no entanto

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Although / however (contrast)
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você expressa contrastes mais formais entre ideias.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "_____ it's cold, I'll go out."
   **Options**: Although / Because / So
   **Answer**: Although
   **Explanation**: "Although" = embora. Introduz contraste/concessão.

2. **multiple_choice** — "I want to travel. _____, I have no money."
   **Options**: However / Because / So
   **Answer**: However
   **Explanation**: "However" = no entanto. Liga frases contrastantes (separadas).

3. **word_bank** — "_____ he's tired, he keeps working."
   **Choices**: Although / Because / So / And
   **Answer**: Although
   **Explanation**: "Although he's tired" — embora cansado.

4. **word_bank** — "She studied a lot. _____, she didn't pass."
   **Choices**: However / Because / Although / And
   **Answer**: However
   **Explanation**: "However" liga duas frases independentes com contraste.

5. **fill_gap** — "_____ I love coffee, I drink tea sometimes."
   **Hint**: Embora (concessão)
   **Answer**: Although
   **Explanation**: "Although I love coffee" — apesar de amar.

6. **fill_gap** — "It's raining. _____, we'll go anyway."
   **Hint**: No entanto (contraste forte)
   **Answer**: However
   **Explanation**: "However, we'll go" — contraste após pausa.

7. **fill_gap** — "_____ she's young, she's very smart."
   **Hint**: Embora
   **Answer**: Although
   **Explanation**: "Although she's young" — concessão.

8. **fix_error** — "Although it's cold but I'll go out."
   **Hint**: Não use although + but juntos
   **Answer**: Although it's cold, I'll go out.
   **Explanation**: "Although" já carrega o contraste — não precisa de "but" também. É erro de dupla conjunção.

9. **fix_error** — "It's raining, however we'll go."
   **Hint**: However precisa de pontuação forte
   **Answer**: It's raining. However, we'll go.
   **Explanation**: "However" normalmente começa nova frase ou após ponto e vírgula. Com vírgula simples soa errado.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "Although I love coffee, I drink tea sometimes. I want to travel. However, I have no money right now."
    **Question**: Why doesn't the speaker travel now?
    **Answer**: no money (or "I have no money")
    **Explanation**: The text says "I have no money right now".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"Although it's cold, I'll go out."** — although
2. **"I want to travel. However, I have no money."** — however
3. **"Although he's tired, he keeps working."** — although
4. **"She studied. However, she didn't pass."** — however
5. **"Although I love coffee, I drink tea sometimes."** — concession
