# Module M16 — Histórias simples

> **Level**: Novice (A2)
> **Block**: A2 Block
> **Units**: 5 (N01–N05)
> **Theme**: Past simple irregular — top 30 verbos
> **Module goal**: Aluno sai sabendo top 30 irregulares (went/saw/did/had/made/came/took/got/etc.) e contar histórias simples no passado.
> **Connects to**: M17 (Não foi assim) — depois de afirmativas irregulares, aprende negativas e perguntas com didn't/Did.

## Module chunks introduced (~30)

- go → went / see → saw / do → did / have → had / make → made
- come → came / take → took / get → got / give → gave / say → said
- buy → bought / bring → brought / think → thought / find → found
- eat → ate / drink → drank / sleep → slept / read → read
- meet → met / leave → left / lose → lost / win → won
- write → wrote / send → sent / put → put / cut → cut
- I went / She had / They came / We saw

## Unit N01 — Eu fui, eu vi, eu fiz

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Top 10 irregulars (go/see/do/have/make/come/take/get/give/say)
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você conta um dia ou viagem usando os 10 irregulares mais usados.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ to São Paulo last year."
   **Options**: went / goed / go
   **Answer**: went
   **Explanation**: "Go" no passado é IRREGULAR: "went" (não "goed"). Decorar.

2. **multiple_choice** — "She _____ a great movie."
   **Options**: saw / seen / seed
   **Answer**: saw
   **Explanation**: "See" → "saw" (irregular). "Seen" é past participle (com have).

3. **word_bank** — "We _____ a lot of fun."
   **Choices**: had / haved / has / having
   **Answer**: had
   **Explanation**: "Have" → "had" (irregular). Mesma forma pra todas as pessoas.

4. **word_bank** — "He _____ a cake yesterday."
   **Choices**: made / maked / makes / making
   **Answer**: made
   **Explanation**: "Make" → "made" (irregular).

5. **fill_gap** — "I _____ home at six."
   **Hint**: Passado de "come"
   **Answer**: came
   **Explanation**: "Come" → "came" (irregular). Note: pronúncia diferente (kAm vs keIm).

6. **fill_gap** — "She _____ a taxi."
   **Hint**: Passado de "take"
   **Answer**: took
   **Explanation**: "Take" → "took" (irregular). Decorar.

7. **fill_gap** — "I _____ your message."
   **Hint**: Passado de "get"
   **Answer**: got
   **Explanation**: "Get" → "got" (irregular).

8. **fix_error** — "I goed to the store."
   **Hint**: Go é irregular
   **Answer**: I went to the store.
   **Explanation**: "Goed" não existe. Past de "go" é "went" (irregular).

9. **fix_error** — "She maked dinner."
   **Hint**: Make é irregular
   **Answer**: She made dinner.
   **Explanation**: "Maked" não existe. "Make" → "made".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "Yesterday I went to a café. I had a coffee and saw an old friend. We took a long walk and made plans for dinner."
    **Question**: What did the speaker have?
    **Answer**: a coffee
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "I had a coffee".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I went to São Paulo."** — go → went
2. **"She saw a movie."** — see → saw
3. **"We had fun."** — have → had
4. **"He made dinner."** — make → made
5. **"I came home late."** — come → came

## Unit N04 — Verbos que não mudam

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Irregulars without change (put/cut/let/read pronunciation)
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você usa verbos cuja forma passada é IGUAL ao infinitivo — só muda o contexto.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ the book on the table."
   **Options**: put / putted / puts
   **Answer**: put
   **Explanation**: "Put" → "put" (igual no passado e presente). Não adiciona -ed.

2. **multiple_choice** — "She _____ her finger yesterday."
   **Options**: cut / cutted / cuts
   **Answer**: cut
   **Explanation**: "Cut" → "cut" (igual). Sem -ed.

3. **word_bank** — "He _____ me know."
   **Choices**: let / letted / lets / letting
   **Answer**: let
   **Explanation**: "Let" → "let" (igual). Sem mudança.

4. **word_bank** — "We _____ the document."
   **Choices**: read / readed / reads / reading
   **Answer**: read
   **Explanation**: "Read" → "read" — IGUAL na escrita, MAS pronúncia muda: /red/ no passado.

5. **fill_gap** — "I _____ my coat in the closet."
   **Hint**: Passado de "put" (igual)
   **Answer**: put
   **Explanation**: "Put" igual ao presente. Sem -ed.

6. **fill_gap** — "She _____ the apple in half."
   **Hint**: Passado de "cut" (igual)
   **Answer**: cut
   **Explanation**: "Cut" igual. Mesma forma.

7. **fill_gap** — "I _____ the article yesterday."
   **Hint**: Passado de "read" — escrita igual, mas atenção à pronúncia
   **Answer**: read
   **Explanation**: "Read" no passado: escrita /read/, mas se pronuncia /red/. Mudança só fonética.

8. **fix_error** — "She putted the book on the shelf."
   **Hint**: Put não muda no passado
   **Answer**: She put the book on the shelf.
   **Explanation**: "Putted" não existe. "Put" no passado = "put" (igual ao presente).

9. **fix_error** — "I cutted my hair."
   **Hint**: Cut não muda
   **Answer**: I cut my hair.
   **Explanation**: "Cutted" não existe. "Cut" → "cut".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I cut the vegetables. Then I put them in the pan. I read the recipe carefully. The result was great."
    **Question**: What did the speaker put in the pan?
    **Answer**: the vegetables
    **Accepts**: them
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "I put them in the pan" — se referindo aos vegetais recém cortados.

2. **"She cut her finger."** — cut unchanged
3. **"He let me know."** — let unchanged

## Unit N05 — Minha história ontem

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Integration top irregulars in narrative
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você conta uma história curta integrando vários verbos irregulares no passado.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ to the park."
   **Options**: went / goed / go
   **Answer**: went
   **Explanation**: Go → went (irregular).

2. **multiple_choice** — "She _____ a movie last night."
   **Options**: saw / seen / sees
   **Answer**: saw
   **Explanation**: See → saw.

3. **word_bank** — "We _____ pizza."
   **Choices**: ate / eated / eats / eating
   **Answer**: ate
   **Explanation**: Eat → ate.

4. **word_bank** — "He _____ a great idea."
   **Choices**: had / haved / has / having
   **Answer**: had
   **Explanation**: Have → had.

5. **fill_gap** — "I _____ a new book."
   **Hint**: Passado de "buy"
   **Answer**: bought
   **Explanation**: Buy → bought.

6. **fill_gap** — "She _____ home at ten."
   **Hint**: Passado de "come"
   **Answer**: came
   **Explanation**: Come → came.

7. **fill_gap** — "I _____ my friend a message."
   **Hint**: Passado de "send"
   **Answer**: sent
   **Explanation**: Send → sent.

8. **fix_error** — "She goed to the party."
   **Hint**: Go é irregular
   **Answer**: She went to the party.
   **Explanation**: Go → went, sempre.

9. **fix_error** — "I eated dinner late."
   **Hint**: Eat é irregular
   **Answer**: I ate dinner late.
   **Explanation**: Eat → ate.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "Yesterday I went to a bookstore. I bought a novel. I came home and read it. I had a great evening!"
    **Question**: What did the speaker buy?
    **Answer**: a novel
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "I bought a novel".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I went to the park."** — went
2. **"She bought a gift."** — bought
3. **"We ate together."** — ate
4. **"He came late."** — came
5. **"I sent the email."** — sent

### 3. Role-play

**Cenário**: Charlotte pede uma história curta de um dia recente, com vários irregulares.
**Tipo**: Live Voice
**Voiced by**: `charlotte`
**Persona**: Charlotte
**Persona outfit**: `charlotte_casual`
**Time budget**: 90s
**Opening line**: "Tell me a short story from yesterday — start with where you went."

**Sub-objectives** (M03+ gradiente: 3 objetivos):

1. **id**: 1
   **label_pt**: "Começar com 'I went to + lugar'"
   **label_en**: "Start with 'I went to'"
   **hidden_prompt**: "user starts with 'I went to + place'"
   **hint_pt**: "I went to the gym."
   **hint_en**: "I went to the gym."

2. **id**: 2
   **label_pt**: "Continuar com outra ação irregular"
   **label_en**: "Continue with another irregular"
   **hidden_prompt**: "user uses another irregular past verb (saw/met/had/bought/etc.)"
   **hint_pt**: "I saw my friend there."
   **hint_en**: "I saw my friend there."

3. **id**: 3
   **label_pt**: "Fechar com outra ação"
   **label_en**: "Close with another action"
   **hidden_prompt**: "user uses one more irregular past"
   **hint_pt**: "We had coffee after."
   **hint_en**: "We had coffee after."

**Closing cue**: Charlotte fecha com "Loved the story!" quando obj_3 baterem.

**Suggested flow** (referencial, 6 turnos):

1. **Charlotte**: "Tell me a short story from yesterday — start with where you went."
2. **Student**: ~"I went to the gym."
3. **Charlotte**: "Then?"
4. **Student**: ~"I saw my friend there."
5. **Charlotte**: "After?"
6. **Student**: ~"We had coffee after."
7. **Charlotte**: "Loved the story!"

**Evaluation focus**:
- Sequência narrativa
- Variedade de irregulares
- Sem voltas pra forma -ed errada

### 4. Guided Chat

**Cenário**: Charlotte conversa sobre uma viagem ou evento que você teve.
**Voiced by**: `charlotte`
**Persona**: Charlotte
**Persona outfit**: `charlotte_casual`
**Intro (em PT)**: "Charlotte abre chat sobre uma experiência passada. Integra os top 30 irregulares."
**Opening message**: "Last big experience — what happened?"

**Sub-objectives** (M03+ gradiente: 3 objetivos):

1. **id**: 1
   **label_pt**: "Começar a história"
   **label_en**: "Start the story"
   **hidden_prompt**: "user starts with 'I went/had/made + something'"
   **hint_pt**: "I went to Italy."
   **hint_en**: "I went to Italy."

2. **id**: 2
   **label_pt**: "Continuar com mais detalhes (irregulares)"
   **label_en**: "Continue with details"
   **hidden_prompt**: "user adds details with irregular past verbs"
   **hint_pt**: "I ate amazing food."
   **hint_en**: "I ate amazing food."

3. **id**: 3
   **label_pt**: "Perguntar à Charlotte"
   **label_en**: "Ask Charlotte"
   **hidden_prompt**: "user asks 'How about you?'"
   **hint_pt**: "How about you?"
   **hint_en**: "How about you?"

**Closing cue**: Charlotte encerra com "Memorable!" quando obj_3 baterem.

**Script** (referencial, 7 turnos):

1. **Charlotte**: "Last big experience — what happened?"
2. **Student**: "I went to Italy."
3. **Charlotte**: "Cool!"
4. **Student**: "I ate amazing food."
5. **Charlotte**: "Yum."
6. **Student**: "How about you?"
7. **Charlotte**: "I went hiking last summer. Memorable!"

## Cross-unit consolidation

Ao terminar M16, o aluno deve usar naturalmente:
- Top 30 verbos irregulares no passado (went/saw/did/had/made/came/etc.)
- Verbos invariáveis (put/cut/let, read pronúncia muda)
- Sem -ed em irregulares
- Sem -s na 3ª pessoa no passado
- Narrativa simples com vários irregulares em sequência

Esses chunks abrem M17 (Não foi assim) — negativas com didn't e perguntas com Did.
