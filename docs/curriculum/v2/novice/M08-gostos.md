# Module M08 — Gostos

> **Level**: Novice (A1)
> **Block**: A1 Block
> **Units**: 5 (N01–N05)
> **Theme**: O que gosto, prefiro, odeio
> **Module goal**: Aluno sai sabendo falar do que gosta/odeia/prefere com like/love/hate + verb-ing, e usar object pronouns (me/you/him/her/it/us/them) em contextos naturais.
> **Connects to**: M09 (Agora mesmo) — depois de gostos estáveis, aprende a falar do que está acontecendo agora (present continuous).

## Module chunks introduced (~35)

- I like / I love / I hate / I prefer
- She likes / he loves / they hate
- like + verb-ing (I like reading)
- love + verb-ing (I love dancing)
- hate + verb-ing (I hate cooking)
- like + noun (I like pizza)
- me / you / him / her / it / us / them (object pronouns)
- I love you / She likes him / Tell me about it
- with me / with us / with them
- What do you like? / What does she like?
- Do you like + verb-ing?
- music / movies / books / sports / food / art

## Unit N01 — Gosto, amo, odeio

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: like/love/hate + noun | **Tense**: PRESENTE · simples
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você conta suas preferências fortes — comidas, filmes, lugares.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ pizza very much."
   **Options**: love / loves / am love
   **Answer**: love
   **Explanation**: Com "I" usa forma base: "I love". "Loves" seria 3ª pessoa. "Am love" não existe.

2. **multiple_choice** — "She _____ chocolate."
   **Options**: loves / love / loving
   **Answer**: loves
   **Explanation**: "She" 3ª pessoa singular → "loves" (love + s).

3. **word_bank** — "I _____ Mondays."
   **Choices**: hate / hates / am hate / no like
   **Answer**: hate
   **Explanation**: "I hate Mondays" = "Odeio segundas". "Hate" forma base com "I". Note: forte mas comum em inglês.

4. **word_bank** — "He _____ Italian food."
   **Choices**: likes / like / liking / is like
   **Answer**: likes
   **Explanation**: "He likes" — 3ª pessoa leva -s. "He" → "likes Italian food".

5. **fill_gap** — "We _____ music very much."
   **Hint**: Verbo "amar" para "we"
   **Answer**: love
   **Explanation**: "We love music" — plural usa forma base.

6. **fill_gap** — "She _____ horror movies."
   **Hint**: Verbo "odiar" 3ª pessoa singular
   **Answer**: hates
   **Explanation**: "She hates" — 3ª pessoa + -s. "Hate" → "hates".

7. **fill_gap** — "I _____ this song!"
   **Hint**: Verbo de afeto forte
   **Answer**: love
   **Explanation**: "I love this song" — afeto forte por música. "Love" + objeto direto.

8. **fix_error** — "She love coffee."
   **Hint**: 3ª pessoa leva -s
   **Answer**: She loves coffee.
   **Explanation**: "She" exige -s no verbo: "She loves coffee", não "She love".

9. **fix_error** — "I am hate vegetables."
   **Hint**: "Hate" não usa "to be"
   **Answer**: I hate vegetables.
   **Explanation**: "Hate" é verbo principal — vai direto após o sujeito. Sem "am/is/are": "I hate", não "I am hate".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I love Italian food. I like Mexican food too. My sister hates spicy food. We all love chocolate."
    **Question**: O que a irmã odeia?
    **Answer**: spicy food
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "My sister hates spicy food".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I love pizza."** — strong affection
2. **"She likes chocolate."** — 3rd person + -s
3. **"I hate Mondays."** — strong dislike
4. **"He loves Italian food."** — 3rd person + love
5. **"We all like music."** — group preference

## Unit N02 — Gosto de fazer

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: like/love/hate + verb-ing | **Tense**: PRESENTE · simples
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você fala de atividades que gosta de fazer — hobbies, esportes, hábitos.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I love _____ music."
   **Options**: listening to / listen to / listen
   **Answer**: listening to
   **Explanation**: Após love/like/hate, verbo no -ing: "love listening to". Note: "listen TO music" (com to). Sem to seria "listen music" (erro).

2. **multiple_choice** — "She likes _____ books."
   **Options**: reading / read / to read
   **Answer**: reading
   **Explanation**: Após "likes", verbo no -ing: "reading". A forma "to read" também é aceitável mas -ing é mais comum em inglês conversacional.

3. **word_bank** — "We hate _____ early."
   **Choices**: waking up / wake up / woke up / waking
   **Answer**: waking up
   **Explanation**: "Hate waking up" — verbo no -ing após hate. "Waking up" mantém o phrasal verb completo.

4. **word_bank** — "He loves _____ soccer."
   **Choices**: playing / play / to play / played
   **Answer**: playing
   **Explanation**: "Loves playing" — verbo no -ing após love. "Play" → "playing".

5. **fill_gap** — "I like _____ in the park."
   **Hint**: Verbo "correr" no gerúndio
   **Answer**: running
   **Explanation**: "I like running" — verbo no -ing após like. "Run" → "running" (dobra o n antes do -ing).

6. **fill_gap** — "She hates _____ the dishes."
   **Hint**: Verbo "lavar" no gerúndio
   **Answer**: washing
   **Explanation**: "Hates washing" — wash + ing = washing. Após hate sempre -ing.

7. **fill_gap** — "We love _____ in the morning."
   **Hint**: Verbo "caminhar" no gerúndio
   **Answer**: walking
   **Explanation**: "Love walking" — walk + ing. Atividade favorita.

8. **fix_error** — "I like read books."
   **Hint**: Após like, verbo no -ing
   **Answer**: I like reading books.
   **Explanation**: Após like/love/hate, verbo SEMPRE no -ing (gerúndio). "Read" → "reading". "I like reading books".

9. **fix_error** — "She loves to dancing."
   **Hint**: Escolha uma forma: -ing ou to + base
   **Answer**: She loves dancing.
   **Explanation**: Ou "She loves dancing" (-ing) OU "She loves to dance" (to + base). NUNCA "to dancing" — misturas as duas formas.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I love reading, but I hate cooking. My brother loves playing soccer. My sister likes painting and drawing."
    **Question**: O que a irmã gosta de fazer?
    **Answer**: painting and drawing
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "My sister likes painting and drawing".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I love reading."** — like + ing
2. **"She likes dancing."** — 3rd person + ing
3. **"We hate waking up early."** — phrasal verb + ing
4. **"He loves playing soccer."** — 3rd person + playing
5. **"I like cooking on weekends."** — activity + when

## Unit N03 — Me, você, ele, ela

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: Object pronouns (me/you/him/her/it/us/them) | **Tense**: PRESENTE · simples
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você fala de relacionamentos e ações entre pessoas — "ela me liga", "eu vejo ele", "nos chamam".

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "She calls _____ every day."
   **Options**: me / I / my
   **Answer**: me
   **Explanation**: "Me" é o objeto (a mim). "I" é o sujeito (eu). "My" é possessivo (meu). Após o verbo, usa "me".

2. **multiple_choice** — "I love _____."
   **Options**: him / he / his
   **Answer**: him
   **Explanation**: "Him" é objeto (a ele). "He" é sujeito. "His" é possessivo. "I love him" = "Eu amo ele".

3. **word_bank** — "Tell _____ about your day."
   **Choices**: me / I / my / mine
   **Answer**: me
   **Explanation**: "Tell me" = "Me conta". "Me" como objeto após verbo. Chunk muito comum.

4. **word_bank** — "We see _____ on Sundays."
   **Choices**: them / they / their / theirs
   **Answer**: them
   **Explanation**: "Them" é objeto plural (a eles/elas). "They" é sujeito. "We see them" = "Nós vemos eles".

5. **fill_gap** — "Ana likes _____."
   **Hint**: Pronome objeto de "you"
   **Answer**: you
   **Explanation**: "You" funciona como sujeito E objeto: "You like Ana" / "Ana likes you". Forma única.

6. **fill_gap** — "Can you help _____?"
   **Hint**: Pronome objeto de "we"
   **Answer**: us
   **Explanation**: "Us" é o objeto de "we". "Help us" = "Ajude a nós/nos ajude".

7. **fill_gap** — "I love this song. Listen to _____!"
   **Hint**: Pronome objeto para coisa
   **Answer**: it
   **Explanation**: "It" para coisas, animais ou conceitos. "Listen to it" = "Ouça (a música)".

8. **fix_error** — "She loves I."
   **Hint**: Após "loves" precisa de pronome objeto
   **Answer**: She loves me.
   **Explanation**: Após verbo, usa pronome objeto. "I" é sujeito. "Me" é objeto. "She loves me", não "She loves I".

9. **fix_error** — "Tell to me about it."
   **Hint**: "Tell" não usa "to" antes do objeto
   **Answer**: Tell me about it.
   **Explanation**: Diferente do português: "tell" vai direto ao objeto sem "to". "Tell me", "Tell him", "Tell us" — sem "to" entre eles.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "My sister calls me every Sunday. I love talking to her. She tells me about her week. I tell her about mine."
    **Question**: Quem liga todo domingo?
    **Answer**: my sister
    **Accepts**: the sister
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "My sister calls me every Sunday".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"She calls me every day."** — object pronoun me
2. **"I love him."** — object pronoun him
3. **"Tell me about it."** — chunk fixo
4. **"We see them on Sundays."** — object pronoun them
5. **"He helps us a lot."** — object pronoun us

### 3. Role-play

**Cenário**: Charlotte quer saber sobre relacionamentos próximos — pessoas importantes na sua vida e o que vocês fazem juntos.
**Tipo**: Live Voice
**Voiced by**: `charlotte`
**Persona**: Charlotte
**Persona outfit**: `charlotte_casual`
**Time budget**: 90s
**Opening line**: "Tell me about people who matter to you."

**Sub-objectives** (M03+ gradiente: 3 objetivos):

1. **id**: 1
   **label_pt**: "Dizer que ama alguém (usando 'him/her')"
   **label_en**: "Say you love someone (using him/her)"
   **hidden_prompt**: "user states 'I love him' or 'I love her' referring to a person"
   **hint_pt**: "I love her."
   **hint_en**: "I love her."

2. **id**: 2
   **label_pt**: "Dizer que alguém te chama ou visita (usando 'me')"
   **label_en**: "Say someone calls or visits you"

   **hint_pt**: "She calls me every day."
   **hint_en**: "She calls me every day."

3. **id**: 3
   **label_pt**: "Dizer que vocês veem alguém juntos (usando 'them')"
   **label_en**: "Say you see someone together (using them)"
   **hidden_prompt**: "user states 'We see them' or 'We visit them' using 'them' as object"
   **hint_pt**: "We see them on Sundays."

- Object pronouns corretos (me/him/her/us/them)

   **hint_pt**: "How about you?"
   **hint_en**: "How about you?"

**Closing cue**: Charlotte encerra com "Beautiful bonds!" quando obj_3 baterem.

## Unit N04 — Música, filmes, esportes

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: like/love/hate + topics (music, movies, books, sports, food, art) | **Tense**: PRESENTE · simples
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você compartilha gostos culturais — música favorita, filmes, séries, esportes.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I love _____ music."
   **Options**: pop / the pop / a pop
   **Answer**: pop
   **Explanation**: Gênero musical sem artigo: "pop music", "rock music", "jazz music". Não "the pop".

2. **multiple_choice** — "She likes _____ movies."
   **Options**: action / actions / an action
   **Answer**: action
   **Explanation**: "Action movies" — substantivo+substantivo (action funciona como adjetivo). Sem -s em "action" mesmo com "movies" plural.

3. **word_bank** — "We hate _____."
   **Choices**: horror movies / horror movie / a horror movie / the horror movies
   **Answer**: horror movies
   **Explanation**: Generalização — "horror movies" no plural sem artigo. "I hate horror movies" = "Odeio filmes de terror em geral".

4. **word_bank** — "He loves _____."
   **Choices**: soccer / a soccer / the soccer / soccers
   **Answer**: soccer
   **Explanation**: Esportes sem artigo nem -s: "soccer", "tennis", "basketball".

5. **fill_gap** — "I love _____ books."
   **Hint**: Tipo de livro (gênero), sem artigo
   **Answer**: reading
   **Explanation**: "I love reading books" — verbo no -ing após love. Ou pode ser tipo: "love mystery books".

6. **fill_gap** — "She listens to _____ music every day."
   **Hint**: Adjetivo de gênero musical
   **Answer**: classical
**Accepts**: pop / rock / jazz
   **Explanation**: "Classical music", "pop music" — gênero + music. Sem artigo.

7. **fill_gap** — "We love _____ — soccer, basketball, tennis."
   **Hint**: Categoria geral
   **Answer**: sports
   **Explanation**: "Sports" — categoria geral. Plural pra indicar variedade. Exemplos: soccer, basketball, tennis.

8. **fix_error** — "I love the music."
   **Hint**: "Music" em geral sem artigo
   **Answer**: I love music.
   **Explanation**: Pra falar de música EM GERAL, sem artigo: "I love music". Com "the" significa "a música" específica.

9. **fix_error** — "She likes a action movies."
   **Hint**: Plural não combina com "a"
   **Answer**: She likes action movies.
   **Explanation**: "A" é singular. Pra plural, sem artigo: "action movies".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I love pop music. I like action movies. I hate horror books. My sister loves classical music — totally different."
    **Question**: Que tipo de música a irmã ama?
    **Answer**: classical
    **Accepts**: classical music
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "My sister loves classical music".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I love pop music."** — music genre
2. **"She likes action movies."** — movie genre + plural
3. **"He hates horror books."** — book genre
4. **"We love sports."** — general sports
5. **"I like classical music."** — different music genre

### 3. Role-play

**Cenário**: Charlotte quer descobrir seus gostos culturais — música, filmes, livros.
**Tipo**: Live Voice
**Voiced by**: `charlotte`
**Persona**: Charlotte
**Persona outfit**: `charlotte_casual`
**Time budget**: 90s
**Opening line**: "Culture check — what kind of music do you love?"

**Sub-objectives** (M03+ gradiente: 3 objetivos):

1. **id**: 1
   **label_pt**: "Dizer um gênero musical que você ama"
   **label_en**: "Say a music genre you love"

## Unit N05 — O que prefiro

> **Sub-CEFR**: A1 | **Grammar focus**: prefer + integration of like/love/hate | **Tense**: PRESENTE · simples
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você compara preferências — "prefiro X em vez de Y".

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ tea over coffee."
   **Options**: prefer / prefers / am prefer
   **Answer**: prefer
   **Explanation**: "I prefer" — forma base com "I". "Prefer + X over Y" = preferir X a Y.

2. **multiple_choice** — "She _____ books to movies."
   **Options**: prefers / prefer / preferring
   **Answer**: prefers
   **Explanation**: "She" 3ª pessoa → "prefers" (prefer + s).

3. **word_bank** — "I prefer _____ to driving."
   **Choices**: walking / walk / walked / a walk
   **Answer**: walking
   **Explanation**: Após "prefer", verbo no -ing. "Walking" (caminhar) preferido a dirigir.

4. **word_bank** — "He prefers _____ at home."
   **Choices**: cooking / cook / to cook / cooked
   **Answer**: cooking
   **Explanation**: Após "prefer" usa -ing. "He prefers cooking" = "Ele prefere cozinhar".

5. **fill_gap** — "I _____ tea, but I drink coffee anyway."
   **Hint**: Verbo "preferir"
   **Answer**: prefer
   **Explanation**: "I prefer tea" = "Prefiro chá". Forma base com "I".

6. **fill_gap** — "She _____ playing tennis to swimming."
   **Hint**: 3ª pessoa singular do verbo "preferir"
   **Answer**: prefers
   **Explanation**: "She prefers playing tennis" — 3ª pessoa + -s.

7. **fill_gap** — "We _____ the beach to the mountains."
   **Hint**: Verbo "preferir" plural
   **Answer**: prefer
   **Explanation**: "We prefer" — plural forma base.

8. **fix_error** — "I prefer to walking."
   **Hint**: Não usa "to" antes de -ing
   **Answer**: I prefer walking.
   **Explanation**: Após "prefer", verbo no -ing OU infinitivo "to + base". Mistura é erro: "to walking" não existe.

9. **fix_error** — "She prefer coffee."
   **Hint**: 3ª pessoa leva -s
   **Answer**: She prefers coffee.
   **Explanation**: "She prefers" — 3ª pessoa singular sempre leva -s.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I prefer tea over coffee. My sister prefers coffee. We both love chocolate. She hates sweet drinks but I like them."
    **Question**: O que a irmã prefere beber?
    **Answer**: coffee
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "My sister prefers coffee".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I prefer tea over coffee."** — comparison preference
2. **"She prefers reading to watching TV."** — 3rd person + activity
3. **"We prefer walking."** — group preference
4. **"He prefers home-cooked food."** — preference + chunk
5. **"I love both, but I prefer the beach."** — mix of love and prefer

## Cross-unit consolidation

Ao terminar M08, o aluno deve usar naturalmente:
- like/love/hate + noun (I love pizza)
- like/love/hate + verb-ing (I love reading)
- Object pronouns: me/you/him/her/it/us/them (She calls me)
- prefer + noun OR verb-ing (I prefer tea over coffee / I prefer walking)
- 3ª pessoa -s em likes/loves/hates/prefers
- Gêneros e categorias sem artigo (pop music, action movies, soccer)
- "Tell me about it" e chunks similares

Esses chunks abrem M09 (Agora mesmo), onde aluno aprende present continuous pra falar do que está acontecendo no momento.
