# Module M14 — Onde estive ontem

> **Level**: Novice (A2)
> **Block**: A2 Block
> **Units**: 5 (N01–N05)
> **Theme**: Passado de "to be" (was/were) + time markers de passado
> **Module goal**: Aluno sai sabendo conjugar was/were (positivo, negativo, perguntas) e usar marcadores de tempo passado (yesterday, last week, two days ago).
> **Connects to**: M15 (O que fiz ontem) — depois de descrever estados passados, fala de AÇÕES passadas com verbos regulares.

## Module chunks introduced (~30)

- I was / you were / he was / she was / it was
- we were / they were
- I wasn't / he wasn't
- you weren't / they weren't
- Was I...? / Was she...? / Were you...? / Were they...?
- Yes, I was. / No, she wasn't.
- yesterday / last night / last week / last month / last year
- two days ago / a year ago / an hour ago
- in 2020 / in January
- at the time / back then

## Unit N01 — Eu estava lá

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: was/were positive
> **Markers**: [denso]
> **Real-life context**: Você conta onde estava e como se sentia em momentos do passado.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ at home yesterday."
   **Options**: was / were / am
   **Answer**: was
   **Explanation**: "I" no passado usa "was". "I was at home" = "Estava em casa".

2. **multiple_choice** — "They _____ at the party."
   **Options**: were / was / are
   **Answer**: were
   **Explanation**: Plurais (they/we/you) usam "were" no passado.

3. **word_bank** — "She _____ tired last night."
   **Choices**: was / were / is / be
   **Answer**: was
   **Explanation**: She/he/it usam "was" no passado.

4. **word_bank** — "We _____ in São Paulo last month."
   **Choices**: were / was / are / be
   **Answer**: were
   **Explanation**: "We" plural usa "were".

5. **fill_gap** — "I _____ very happy yesterday."
   **Hint**: Passado de "am"
   **Answer**: was
   **Explanation**: "I was happy" — passado de "I am". "Was" pra I/he/she/it.

6. **fill_gap** — "They _____ at the cinema last night."
   **Hint**: Passado de "are" para plural
   **Answer**: were
   **Explanation**: "They were" — plural sempre "were".

7. **fill_gap** — "Maria _____ a student two years ago."
   **Hint**: Passado de "is"
   **Answer**: was
   **Explanation**: Maria = she → "was". 3ª pessoa singular passada.

8. **fix_error** — "We was at the park."
   **Hint**: Plural usa "were"
   **Answer**: We were at the park.
   **Explanation**: "We" plural → "were", não "was". Apenas I/he/she/it usam "was".

9. **fix_error** — "She were tired."
   **Hint**: She é singular
   **Answer**: She was tired.
   **Explanation**: She/he/it → "was" (singular). "Were" é pra plurais e "you".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "Yesterday I was at home. My family was with me. We were happy together. It was a great day."
    **Question**: Onde o speaker estava ontem?
    **Answer**: at home
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "I was at home".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I was at home yesterday."** — own past location
2. **"They were at the party."** — plural past
3. **"She was tired last night."** — 3rd person past
4. **"We were happy together."** — plural we past
5. **"It was a great day."** — it + was

## Unit N03 — Você estava lá?

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: was/were questions
> **Markers**: [qform]
> **Real-life context**: Você pergunta sobre onde alguém estava e como estava.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "_____ you at home yesterday?"
   **Options**: Were / Was / Are
   **Answer**: Were
   **Explanation**: "Were + you" — "you" usa "were" no passado. Inversão direta na pergunta.

2. **multiple_choice** — "_____ she happy?"
   **Options**: Was / Were / Did
   **Answer**: Was
   **Explanation**: "Was + she" — she usa "was". Inversão no passado.

3. **word_bank** — "_____ they at the party?"
   **Choices**: Were / Was / Did / Have
   **Answer**: Were
   **Explanation**: "Were they?" — plural usa "were".

4. **word_bank** — "Where _____ you yesterday?"
   **Choices**: were / was / did / have
   **Answer**: were
   **Explanation**: "Where were you?" — WH + were + sujeito.

5. **fill_gap** — "_____ he at the cinema?"
   **Hint**: Auxiliar para he no passado
   **Answer**: Was
   **Explanation**: "Was he?" — he/she/it com "was" em pergunta.

6. **fill_gap** — "How _____ your weekend?"
   **Hint**: Auxiliar para "your weekend"
   **Answer**: was
   **Explanation**: "How was your weekend?" — singular usa "was".

7. **fill_gap** — "Yes, I _____." (responda a "Were you tired?")
   **Hint**: Resposta curta afirmativa
   **Answer**: was
   **Explanation**: "Yes, I was" — short answer com to be passado.

8. **fix_error** — "Did you at home?"
   **Hint**: "To be" no passado usa was/were
   **Answer**: Were you at home?
   **Explanation**: Pergunta com "to be" passado usa was/were, não "did". "Did" é pra outros verbos.

9. **fix_error** — "Was they happy?"
   **Hint**: They é plural
   **Answer**: Were they happy?
   **Explanation**: They → "were", não "was". Plural sempre "were".

    **Explanation**: O texto diz "She was sick" — explicando por que ela não estava na reunião.

2. **"Was she happy?"** — 3rd person question

## Unit N04 — Ontem, semana passada

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Time markers of the past (yesterday, last X, X ago, in YEAR)
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você usa marcadores temporais pra ancorar eventos passados na linha do tempo.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I was at home _____."
   **Options**: yesterday / today / tomorrow
   **Answer**: yesterday
   **Explanation**: "Yesterday" = passado (ontem). "Today" = hoje, "tomorrow" = amanhã.

2. **multiple_choice** — "We were in Italy _____ week."
   **Options**: last / next / this
   **Answer**: last
   **Explanation**: "Last week" = semana passada. "Next" = futuro; "this" = atual.

3. **word_bank** — "She was here two hours _____."
   **Choices**: ago / before / past / since
   **Answer**: ago
   **Explanation**: "Two hours ago" = duas horas atrás. "Ago" sempre depois do tempo, indica passado.

4. **word_bank** — "I was born _____ 1990."
   **Choices**: in / on / at / from
   **Answer**: in
   **Explanation**: "In + ano": "in 1990", "in 2020". Não confundir com "on + date" (on the 5th).

5. **fill_gap** — "I was tired _____ night."
   **Hint**: Combina com "night" pra passado próximo
   **Answer**: last
   **Explanation**: "Last night" = ontem à noite. "Last + Monday/week/month/year" também.

6. **fill_gap** — "We were friends three years _____."
   **Hint**: Marcador "atrás"
   **Answer**: ago
   **Explanation**: "Three years ago" = três anos atrás. Posição: depois do tempo.

7. **fill_gap** — "She was in Paris _____ January."
   **Hint**: Preposição com mês
   **Answer**: in
   **Explanation**: "In + month": in January, in February.

8. **fix_error** — "I was here ago two hours."
   **Hint**: Posição de "ago"
   **Answer**: I was here two hours ago.
   **Explanation**: "Ago" SEMPRE depois do tempo: "two hours ago", "three days ago". Nunca antes.

9. **fix_error** — "We were on vacation at January."
   **Hint**: Preposição errada com mês
   **Answer**: We were on vacation in January.
   **Explanation**: Meses usam "in", não "at". "In January", "in summer".

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "I was in São Paulo last month. Two years ago I was in Rio. In 2020 I was in Lisbon."
    **Question**: Quando o speaker esteve em Lisbon?
    **Answer**: in 2020
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "In 2020 I was in Lisbon".

### 2. Listening/Speaking (5 phrases)

1. **"I was at home yesterday."** — yesterday
2. **"We were in Italy last week."** — last week
3. **"She was here two hours ago."** — X ago
4. **"I was born in 1990."** — in + year
5. **"He was at the party last night."** — last night

## Unit N05 — Era assim antes

> **Sub-CEFR**: A2 | **Grammar focus**: Integration was/were + descriptions of past states
> **Markers**: —
> **Real-life context**: Você descreve como era sua vida antes — onde, com quem, como.

### 1. Grammar (10 exercises)

1. **multiple_choice** — "I _____ a child in 2010."
   **Options**: was / were / am
   **Answer**: was
   **Explanation**: "I was a child in 2010" — passado de I. Was + sujeito singular.

2. **multiple_choice** — "We _____ neighbors back then."
   **Options**: were / was / are
   **Answer**: were
   **Explanation**: "We were neighbors" — passado plural.

3. **word_bank** — "She _____ very young."
   **Choices**: was / were / is / be
   **Answer**: was
   **Explanation**: She → "was". Passado singular.

4. **word_bank** — "I _____ tired and sad."
   **Choices**: was / were / am / had
   **Answer**: was
   **Explanation**: "I was tired and sad" — passado de "I am".

5. **fill_gap** — "It _____ a long time ago."
   **Hint**: Passado de "it is"
   **Answer**: was
   **Explanation**: "It was" — passado de "it is".

6. **fill_gap** — "They _____ best friends in school."
   **Hint**: Passado plural
   **Answer**: were
   **Explanation**: "They were best friends" — passado plural.

7. **fill_gap** — "_____ you happy back then?"
   **Hint**: Pergunta com "you" no passado
   **Answer**: Were
   **Explanation**: "Were you happy?" — pergunta com you no passado.

8. **fix_error** — "I were happy when I was young."
   **Hint**: I no passado usa was
   **Answer**: I was happy when I was young.
   **Explanation**: I → "was", não "were". Mesmo em frases longas, mantém was.

9. **fix_error** — "They was at the party."
   **Hint**: They é plural
   **Answer**: They were at the party.
   **Explanation**: They → "were", não "was". Plural sempre were.

10. **read_answer**
    **Passage**: "When I was a child, we were poor but happy. My parents were always busy. It was a simple life."
    **Question**: Como estavam os pais?
    **Answer**: always busy
    **Accepts**: busy
    **Explanation**: O texto diz "My parents were always busy".

## Cross-unit consolidation

Ao terminar M14, o aluno deve usar naturalmente:
- was (I/he/she/it) vs were (we/you/they)
- wasn't / weren't (contraídas)
- Was/Were + sujeito + ...? (perguntas)
- Time markers: yesterday, last week, two days ago, in 2020
- Posição: "ago" depois, "in" antes do ano

Esses chunks abrem M15 (O que fiz ontem), onde o aluno aprende verbos regulares no passado (-ed).
