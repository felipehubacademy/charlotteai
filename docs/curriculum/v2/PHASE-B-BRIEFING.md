## PARTE 1 — 5 MÓDULOS NOVOS

### M23 Novice — Em viagem, no médico, ao telefone

**Arquivo**: `docs/curriculum/v2/novice/M23-sobrevivencia.md`
**Tema**: Survival real — situações que o aluno A2 enfrenta viajando ou no dia-a-dia.
**Sub-CEFR**: A2 (cumulativo M01-M22)
**Goal**: Aluno sobrevive em situações reais — pede informação no aeroporto, hotel, fala com médico básico, atende telefone em inglês.
**Connects to**: Inter I23 (tag questions reforçam conversação real).

**Module chunks** (~30):
- "Where is the gate / boarding / bathroom?"
- "I'd like to check in / check out"
- "Do you have a room available?"
- "I'm here for [business / vacation]"
- "How do I get to [place]?"
- "Excuse me, can you help me?"
- "I lost my luggage / passport"
- "My flight is delayed / cancelled"
- "I don't feel well"
- "I have a headache / stomachache / fever"
- "Can you call a doctor?"
- "Where's the pharmacy?"
- "I need [medicine / water / a doctor]"
- "Hello, this is [name] speaking"
- "Can I speak to [person]?"
- "Can I leave a message?"
- "Sorry, wrong number"
- "Hold on a second"
- "I'll call you back"
- "Could you repeat that?"
- "Speak slower, please"
- (full list de chunks de fala real)

**Units propostas**:
- N01 "No aeroporto" — check-in, boarding, gates, lost luggage
- N02 "No hotel" — check-in/out, room types, problems with the room
- N03 "Pedindo direções" — "Where is...", "How do I get to...", "Turn left/right"
- N04 "Não me sinto bem" — body parts review + symptoms + at the doctor
- N05 "Ao telefone" — Phone English completo: open, identify, request, hold, message, close

**Grammar permitida**: Apenas M01-M22 cumulativo. Heavy uso de present simple, can/could (M07), would like (chunks M11), there is/are (M04), prepositions of place (M11).

### M24 Novice — Reagindo na conversa

**Arquivo**: `docs/curriculum/v2/novice/M24-reagindo-na-conversa.md`
**Tema**: Reações curtas conversacionais — destrava o aluno em conversas reais. Hoje só aparece em Advanced A21; mover essência pra Novice porque é survival conversational.
**Sub-CEFR**: A2 (cumulativo M01-M23)
**Goal**: Aluno reage naturalmente em conversação (não fica mudo enquanto pensa o que dizer) usando chunks de high-frequency.
**Connects to**: I23 (fillers Inter expandem isso).

**Module chunks** (~30):
- "Cool" / "Sweet" / "Awesome" / "Nice"
- "Right" / "Got it" / "Sure" / "Okay"
- "No way!" / "Get out!" / "For real?" / "Seriously?" / "Are you kidding?"
- "Hold on" / "Hang on" / "Wait" / "Hold up"
- "Really?" / "Oh yeah?" / "Is that so?"
- "Bummer" / "That sucks" / "Oh no"
- "Whatever" / "Never mind" / "No biggie"
- "I see" / "I know" / "I get it" / "Makes sense"
- "Same here" / "Me too" / "Me neither"
- "Oh!" / "Hmm" / "Yeah..." (acknowledgment sounds)
- "Anyway..." / "By the way..." / "So..."
- "What?" / "Huh?" / "Excuse me?"

**Units propostas**:
- N01 "Mostrando interesse" — Really?, Oh yeah?, Tell me more, That's interesting
- N02 "Surpresa e descrença" — No way!, Get out!, Seriously?, Are you kidding?, For real?
- N03 "Confirmando entendimento" — Got it, Makes sense, I see, Right
- N04 "Reação a más notícias" — Bummer, That sucks, Oh no, I'm sorry to hear that
- N05 "Pausas e transições" — Hold on, Hang on, Wait, By the way, Anyway

**Grammar**: Cumulativo M01-M23. Chunks de fala — Grammar exercises focam em selecionar a reação certa para o contexto.

### I23 Inter — Tag questions + fillers

**Arquivo**: `docs/curriculum/v2/inter/M23-tag-questions-fillers.md`
**Tema**: Tag questions sistemáticas (verbo to be, do/does/did, have/has, modais, casos especiais) + fillers conversacionais básicos (you know, I mean, like, well).
**Sub-CEFR**: B1/B2 (cumulativo I01-I22)
**Goal**: Aluno usa tag questions corretamente em conversação + emprega fillers naturalmente sem soar inseguro.
**Connects to**: A09 (Advanced idioms) e A21 (native fluency) — extends conversational naturalness.

**Module chunks** (~30):
- "You like coffee, don't you?"
- "She's studying, isn't she?"
- "They were here, weren't they?"
- "You can swim, can't you?"
- "You've seen it, haven't you?"
- "I am right, aren't I?" (caso especial)
- "Let's go, shall we?" (caso especial)
- "Open the door, will you?" (imperativo)
- "Nobody knows, do they?" (com indefinidos)
- "You know..." / "I mean..." / "Like..."
- "Well..." / "So..." / "Right..."
- "Kind of" / "Sort of" / "Pretty much"
- "Actually..." / "Basically..." / "Obviously..."
- "I guess..." / "I suppose..." / "I'd say..."

**Units propostas**:
- I01 "Tags com to be e have" — present/past be, have/has variants
- I02 "Tags com do/does/did" — auxiliary do family
- I03 "Tags com modais" — can/could/will/would/should
- I04 "Casos especiais" — I am → aren't I; let's → shall we; imperatives → will you?; com indefinidos (everyone → don't they)
- I05 "Fillers naturais" — você know, I mean, like, well + intonação rising/falling de tags (confirm vs check)

**Grammar**: Inter B1/B2 cumulativo. Heavy use of auxiliaries (todos os tempos já cobertos).

### A23 Advanced — Business & email writing

**Arquivo**: `docs/curriculum/v2/advanced/M23-business-email-writing.md`
**Tema**: Inglês profissional escrito — emails formais, candidaturas, cover letters, follow-ups, registros workplace.
**Sub-CEFR**: C1 (cumulativo A01-A22, com peso em A12 academic + A11 register)
**Goal**: Aluno escreve emails profissionais, candidaturas, follow-ups e mensagens workplace com registro adequado, hedging e clarity.
**Connects to**: A12 (academic writing) já cobre escrita formal; A23 é workplace-specific.

**Module chunks** (~25):
- Subject lines: "Following up on...", "Re: [topic]", "Quick question about..."
- Openings formais: "I hope this email finds you well", "I'm reaching out regarding..."
- Openings semi-formais: "Hope you're doing well", "Quick note about..."
- Body chunks: "I'd like to..." / "I'd appreciate it if..." / "Would it be possible to..."
- Cover letter chunks: "I am writing to apply for...", "I bring [X years] of experience in...", "I'd welcome the opportunity to discuss..."
- Follow-up chunks: "Just following up on...", "Wanted to check in on...", "Any update on...?"
- Hedging: "It might be worth considering...", "Perhaps we could...", "I'd suggest..."
- Closings: "Best regards", "Kind regards", "Best", "Thanks again"
- Bad news softening: "Unfortunately...", "I regret to inform you...", "Unfortunately, at this time..."
- Apologizing: "Apologies for the delay...", "I appreciate your patience"

**Units propostas**:
- A01 "Email openings & closings" — formais vs semi-formais; quando usar qual
- A02 "Job applications" — opening line, body structure, why-me paragraph, closing
- A03 "Cover letters & CVs language" — chunks pra resume bullets, achievements quantification
- A04 "Follow-ups & check-ins" — após meeting, após application, após silence
- A05 "Difficult emails" — declining politely, delivering bad news, asking for raise/promotion

**Grammar**: Advanced cumulativo. Heavy em hedging (A08), passive (A05), modais (A19), discourse markers (A10).

### A24 Advanced — Idioms culturais

**Arquivo**: `docs/curriculum/v2/advanced/M24-idioms-culturais.md`
**Tema**: Idiomas culturais altamente idiomáticos — expressões cuja origem é cultural e cujo significado não pode ser deduzido das palavras. Diferente de A09 (idioms gerais) por focar em expressões com forte carga cultural anglo-saxã.
**Sub-CEFR**: C2 (cumulativo A01-A23)
**Goal**: Aluno reconhece e usa apropriadamente idioms culturais de high-frequency em literatura, mídia e conversa nativa.
**Connects to**: A09 (idioms gerais), A14 (literary language), A21 (native fluency).

**Module chunks** (~30, selecionados por frequência real):
- "kick the bucket" (die)
- "spill the beans" (reveal a secret)
- "hit the nail on the head" (be exactly right)
- "break the ice" (start a conversation)
- "bite the bullet" (do something unpleasant)
- "cost an arm and a leg" (very expensive)
- "let the cat out of the bag" (reveal a secret)
- "piece of cake" (very easy)
- "once in a blue moon" (rarely)
- "the elephant in the room" (obvious unaddressed topic)
- "burn bridges" (destroy relationships)
- "throw in the towel" (give up)
- "barking up the wrong tree" (mistaken direction)
- "the ball is in your court" (your turn to act)
- "cutting corners" (taking shortcuts)
- "behind the scenes" (in private/hidden)
- "down to earth" (humble/practical)
- "a blessing in disguise" (good thing in bad form)
- "rule of thumb" (general principle)
- "speak of the devil" (when someone mentioned appears)
- "the last straw" (final issue causing breakdown)
- "wear your heart on your sleeve" (show emotions openly)
- "burn the midnight oil" (work late)
- "out of the blue" (suddenly)
- "Pandora's box" (uncontrollable consequences)

**Units propostas**:
- A01 "Idiomas com partes do corpo" — kick the bucket, cost arm and leg, wear heart on sleeve, give cold shoulder, bite the bullet
- A02 "Idiomas com animais" — spill the beans (origem), barking wrong tree, elephant in room, when pigs fly, cat out of bag
- A03 "Idiomas de tempo e raridade" — once in blue moon, burn midnight oil, time flies, out of the blue, rain check
- A04 "Idiomas de decisão e ação" — bite the bullet, throw in towel, ball is in your court, cut corners, last straw
- A05 "Idiomas conversacionais" — speak of the devil, piece of cake, break the ice, hit nail on head, rule of thumb

**Grammar**: Cumulativo total. Foco em USO contextual, não em conjugação.

### 1. Novice M05 (Meu dia-a-dia) → +N06 "Que horas? Que dia?"

**Arquivo**: `docs/curriculum/v2/novice/M05-meu-dia-a-dia.md` (adicionar Unit N06 ao final)
**Tema**: Telling time + dates + days/months/seasons básicas
**Chunks**: "What time is it?", "It's [X] o'clock", "quarter past/to", "half past", "at noon/midnight", "in the morning/afternoon/evening/night", days of the week, months, "What day is it?", "It's [Monday]", "the [Nth] of [month]", "today/tomorrow/yesterday"

### 2. Novice M19 (O futuro) → +N06 "Tempo e estações"

**Arquivo**: `docs/curriculum/v2/novice/M19-o-futuro.md`
**Tema**: Weather vocabulary + forecast com `will`
**Chunks**: "It's hot/cold/sunny/rainy/cloudy/windy/snowing", "It's going to rain tomorrow", "What's the weather like?", "It'll be sunny", "It's freezing/boiling", seasons (spring/summer/fall/autumn/winter), weather small talk britânica

### 3. Novice M18 (Planos pra amanhã) → +N06 "Listening: gonna, wanna, gotta"

**Arquivo**: `docs/curriculum/v2/novice/M18-planos-pra-amanha.md`
**Tema**: Listening RECEPTIVO de reduções coloquiais — aluno aprende a DECODIFICAR áudio nativo, não necessariamente reproduzir.
**Chunks**: "I'm gonna [eat]" = "I'm going to eat", "I wanna [go]" = "I want to go", "I gotta [leave]" = "I've got to leave", "Whatcha doing?" = "What are you doing?", "D'you wanna...?"
**Notas pedagógicas**: Listening-first. Grammar exercises são de "match the spoken form to the formal form". L&S phrases em áudio CDN devem usar essas reduções no áudio (gen-time o TTS pode aplicar). Role-play e Chat usam formal `going to` ainda — Speaking nativa fica pra Inter/Advanced.

### 4. Inter I05 (Phrasal verbs essenciais) → +I06 "Phone English"

**Arquivo**: `docs/curriculum/v2/inter/M05-phrasal-verbs-essenciais.md`
**Tema**: Telefone em registro Inter — phrasals e expressões de chamadas
**Chunks**: "Hello, this is [name]", "Can I speak to [person]?", "Speaking" (yes, it's me), "Hold on" / "Hang on" / "Hold the line", "I'll put you through", "She's on another line", "Can I take a message?", "Can I call you back?", "We were cut off", "I'm running late", "I'll get back to you", "Could you spell that?"
**Justificativa de I05**: phrasals + phone fluem juntos (pick up, hang up, put through, call back, get back).

### 5. Inter I22 (Conectores avançados) → +I06 "Trabalho expandido"

**Arquivo**: `docs/curriculum/v2/inter/M22-conectores-avancados.md`
**Tema**: Workplace English fluente — vocab profissional + linking
**Chunks**: "boss / supervisor / manager / colleague / coworker", "deadline / due date / target", "meeting / call / sync", "salary / paycheck / raise / bonus", "office / workspace / remote / hybrid", "promotion / demotion / fire / quit / resign", "I work for [company]" vs "I work at [location]", "What do you do for a living?", "I'm in [industry]"
**Justificativa de I22**: conectores são para discurso profissional formal; encaixar vocab workplace expande o uso.

### 6. Advanced A11 (Conotação e registro) → +A06 "UK vs US + Falsos cognatos cross-link"

**Arquivo**: `docs/curriculum/v2/advanced/M11-conotacao-registro.md`
**Tema**: Awareness UK vs US (vocab + spelling + gotten/got) + reforço final de falsos cognatos pra lusófonos
**Chunks UK/US**: elevator/lift, truck/lorry, apartment/flat, line/queue, sidewalk/pavement, vacation/holiday, fall/autumn, math/maths, gotten/got, color/colour
**Falsos cognatos refresh**: push, pretend, library, parents, college, actually, eventually, realize, advise, sensible, intend, attend, assist, exit
**Notas**: Unit awareness-style — não exige produção, apenas reconhecimento e contexto.
